Graduate Psychology Education

Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA) · Assistance Listing 93.191

HRSA obligated $97M under this program to 66 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 22 new grants and cooperative agreements to 21 institutions, with a median of $450K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 48% were R1s, 19% R2s and 10% RCUs.
