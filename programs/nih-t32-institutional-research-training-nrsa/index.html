NIH T32: Institutional research training (NRSA)

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code T32, all institutes

NIH awarded $3.13B in total cost under activity code T32 to 189 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 979 new and renewal awards to 151 institutions, with a median of $374K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 81% were R1s, 16% R2s and 3% RCUs.
