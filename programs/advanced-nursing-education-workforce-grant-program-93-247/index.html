Advanced Nursing Education Workforce Grant Program

Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA) · Assistance Listing 93.247

HRSA obligated $307M under this program to 117 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 9 new grants and cooperative agreements to 9 institutions, with a median of $500K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 22% were R1s, 44% R2s and 22% RCUs.
