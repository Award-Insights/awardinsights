EPSCoR Co-Funding

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 915000 · Math & Physical Sci

NSF made $288M in awards (full value) under this program to 143 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 454 awards to 97 institutions, with a median of $509K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 58% were R1s, 22% R2s and 12% RCUs.
