Coastal Zone Management Estuarine Research Reserves

Commerce (DOC) · Assistance Listing 11.420

DOC obligated $63M under this program to 47 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 68 new grants and cooperative agreements to 34 institutions, with a median of $322K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 82% were R1s, 12% R2s and 6% RCUs.
