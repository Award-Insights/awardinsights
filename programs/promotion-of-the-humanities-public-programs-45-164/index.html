Promotion of the Humanities Public Programs

National Endowment for the Humanities (NEH) · Assistance Listing 45.164

NEH obligated $29M under this program to 57 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 33 new grants and cooperative agreements to 29 institutions, with a median of $75K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 59% were R1s, 3% R2s and 3% RCUs.
