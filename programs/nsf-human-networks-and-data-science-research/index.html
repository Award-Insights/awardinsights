Human Networks & Data Science Research

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 147Y00 · Social/Behav/Econ

NSF made $13M in awards (full value) under this program to 57 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 28 awards to 27 institutions, with a median of $379K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 74% were R1s, 7% R2s and 11% RCUs.
