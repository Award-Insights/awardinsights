NSF Convergence Accelerator

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 131Y00 · Tech, Innov & Partnerships

NSF made $203M in awards (full value) under this program to 67 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 103 awards to 59 institutions, with a median of $750K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 92% were R1s, 5% R2s and 2% RCUs.
