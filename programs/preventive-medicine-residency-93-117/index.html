Preventive Medicine Residency

Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA) · Assistance Listing 93.117

HRSA obligated $28M under this program to 20 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2021–23, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 16 new grants and cooperative agreements to 15 institutions, with a median of $400K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 73% were R1s, 20% R2s and 7% RCUs.
