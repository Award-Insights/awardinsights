ECR: Building Capacity in STEM Education Research (ECR:BCSER)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 162Y00 · STEM Education

NSF made $39M in awards (full value) under this program to 71 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 73 awards to 58 institutions, with a median of $350K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 79% were R1s, 17% R2s and 3% RCUs.
