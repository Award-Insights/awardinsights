NIH S10: Shared instrumentation

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code S10, all institutes

NIH awarded $476M in total cost under activity code S10 to 211 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 407 new and renewal awards to 173 institutions, with a median of $563K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 56% were R1s, 21% R2s and 11% RCUs.
