NIH P40: Animal model resource

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code P40, all institutes

NIH awarded $85M in total cost under activity code P40 to 16 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 12 new and renewal awards to 11 institutions, with a median of $843K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 55% were R1s, 27% R2s and 18% RCUs.
