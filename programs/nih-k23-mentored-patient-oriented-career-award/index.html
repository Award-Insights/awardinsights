NIH K23: Mentored patient-oriented career award

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code K23, all institutes

NIH awarded $911M in total cost under activity code K23 to 127 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 625 new and renewal awards to 84 institutions, with a median of $189K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 84% were R1s, 13% R2s and 2% RCUs.
