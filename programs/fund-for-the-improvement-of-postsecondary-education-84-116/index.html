Fund for the Improvement of Postsecondary Education

Education (ED) · Assistance Listing 84.116

ED obligated $605M under this program to 262 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 191 new grants and cooperative agreements to 173 institutions, with a median of $2.0M (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 24% were R1s, 15% R2s and 8% RCUs.
