Physiological Mechanisms and Biomechanics

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 765800 · Bio Sciences

NSF made $65M in awards (full value) under this program to 92 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 58 awards to 47 institutions, with a median of $685K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 74% were R1s, 11% R2s and 6% RCUs.
