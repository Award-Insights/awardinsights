Defense (DOD) · Assistance Listing 12.902

DOD obligated $42M under this program to 72 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 82 new grants and cooperative agreements to 73 institutions, with a median of $136K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 35% were R1s, 24% R2s and 15% RCUs.
