NIH F99: Predoctoral-to-postdoctoral transition

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code F99, all institutes

NIH awarded $19M in total cost under activity code F99 to 106 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 108 new and renewal awards to 64 institutions, with a median of $44K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 87% were R1s, 11% R2s and 2% RCUs.
