Mental Health Research Grants

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · Assistance Listing 93.242

NIH obligated $7.28B under this program to 280 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 1,863 new grants and cooperative agreements to 193 institutions, with a median of $430K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 76% were R1s, 17% R2s and 3% RCUs.
