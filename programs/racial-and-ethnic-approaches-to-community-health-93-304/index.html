Racial and Ethnic Approaches to Community Health

Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) · Assistance Listing 93.304

CDC obligated $33M under this program to 11 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2021–23, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 18 new grants and cooperative agreements to 11 institutions, with a median of $976K.
