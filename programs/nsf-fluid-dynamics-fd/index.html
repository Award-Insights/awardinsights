Fluid Dynamics (FD)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 144300 · Engineering

NSF made $75M in awards (full value) under this program to 111 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 134 awards to 83 institutions, with a median of $344K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 87% were R1s, 10% R2s and 4% RCUs.
