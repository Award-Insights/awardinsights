Nurse Education, Practice Quality and Retention Grants

Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA) · Assistance Listing 93.359

HRSA obligated $279M under this program to 111 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 60 new grants and cooperative agreements to 59 institutions, with a median of $753K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 31% were R1s, 12% R2s and 14% RCUs.
