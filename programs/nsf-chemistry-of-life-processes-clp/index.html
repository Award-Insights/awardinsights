National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 688300 · Math & Physical Sci

NSF made $103M in awards (full value) under this program to 124 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 106 awards to 78 institutions, with a median of $576K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 82% were R1s, 4% R2s and 4% RCUs.
