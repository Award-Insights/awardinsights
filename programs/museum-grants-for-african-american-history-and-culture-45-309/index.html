Museum Grants for African American History and Culture

Institute of Museum and Library Services (IMLS) · Assistance Listing 45.309

IMLS obligated $6.9M under this program to 26 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 24 new grants and cooperative agreements to 18 institutions, with a median of $100K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 6% were R1s, 39% R2s and 22% RCUs.
