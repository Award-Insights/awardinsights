NIH U01: Research project, cooperative

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code U01, all institutes

NIH awarded $7.42B in total cost under activity code U01 to 217 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 537 new and renewal awards to 115 institutions, with a median of $806K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 86% were R1s, 12% R2s and 2% RCUs.
