National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 128100 · Math & Physical Sci

NSF made $107M in awards (full value) under this program to 136 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 319 awards to 117 institutions, with a median of $200K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 85% were R1s, 9% R2s and 5% RCUs.
