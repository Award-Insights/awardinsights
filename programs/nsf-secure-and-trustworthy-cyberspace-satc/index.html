Secure and Trustworthy Cyberspace (SaTC)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 806000 · Computer & Info Sci

NSF made $375M in awards (full value) under this program to 211 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 480 awards to 151 institutions, with a median of $399K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 80% were R1s, 15% R2s and 5% RCUs.
