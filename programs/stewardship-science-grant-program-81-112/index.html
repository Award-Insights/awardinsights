Stewardship Science Grant Program

Energy (DOE) · Assistance Listing 81.112

DOE obligated $641M under this program to 40 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 31 new grants and cooperative agreements to 22 institutions, with a median of $300K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 100% were R1s.
