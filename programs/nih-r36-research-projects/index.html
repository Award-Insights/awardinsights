NIH R36: Research projects

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code R36, all institutes

NIH awarded $9.5M in total cost under activity code R36 to 64 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 68 new and renewal awards to 41 institutions, with a median of $52K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 93% were R1s and 7% R2s.
