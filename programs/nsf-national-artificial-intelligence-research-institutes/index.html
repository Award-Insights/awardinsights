National Artificial Intelligence Research Institutes

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 132Y00 · Computer & Info Sci

NSF made $319M in awards (full value) under this program to 31 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 23 awards to 17 institutions, with a median of $15M (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 100% were R1s.
