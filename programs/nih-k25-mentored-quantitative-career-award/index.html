NIH K25: Mentored quantitative career award

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code K25, all institutes

NIH awarded $34M in total cost under activity code K25 to 50 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 25 new and renewal awards to 19 institutions, with a median of $143K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 84% were R1s, 11% R2s and 5% RCUs.
