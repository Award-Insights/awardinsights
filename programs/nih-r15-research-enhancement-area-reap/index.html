NIH R15: Research enhancement (AREA/REAP)

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code R15, all institutes

NIH awarded $551M in total cost under activity code R15 to 321 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 649 new and renewal awards to 242 institutions, with a median of $462K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 22% were R1s, 28% R2s and 27% RCUs.
