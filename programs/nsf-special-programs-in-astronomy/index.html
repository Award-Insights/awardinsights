Special Programs in Astronomy

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 121900 · Math & Physical Sci

NSF made $19M in awards (full value) under this program to 41 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 29 awards to 27 institutions, with a median of $375K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 74% were R1s, 11% R2s and 7% RCUs.
