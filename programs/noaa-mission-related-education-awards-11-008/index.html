NOAA Mission-Related Education Awards

Commerce (DOC) · Assistance Listing 11.008

DOC obligated $12M under this program to 19 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 12 new grants and cooperative agreements to 11 institutions, with a median of $100K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 64% were R1s, 18% R2s and 9% RCUs.
