Tribal Colleges and Universities Program (TCUP)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 174400 · STEM Education

NSF made $95M in awards (full value) under this program to 31 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 49 awards to 24 institutions, with a median of $538K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 4% were R1s, 4% R2s and 8% RCUs.
