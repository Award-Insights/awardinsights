NIH U24: Resource-related, cooperative

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code U24, all institutes

NIH awarded $3.26B in total cost under activity code U24 to 114 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 190 new and renewal awards to 71 institutions, with a median of $799K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 88% were R1s, 9% R2s and 3% RCUs.
