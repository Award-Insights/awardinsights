Research Experiences and Mentoring for Postbaccalaureates (RaMP)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 201Y00 · Bio Sciences

NSF made $53M in awards (full value) under this program to 19 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2021–23, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 19 awards to 19 institutions, with a median of $3.0M. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 74% were R1s and 21% R2s.
