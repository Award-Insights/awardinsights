Research Infrastructure Programs

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · Assistance Listing 93.351

NIH obligated $1.17B under this program to 183 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 446 new grants and cooperative agreements to 145 institutions, with a median of $453K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 78% were R1s, 15% R2s and 5% RCUs.
