Graduate Assistance in Areas of National Need

Education (ED) · Assistance Listing 84.200

ED obligated $113M under this program to 70 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 73 new grants and cooperative agreements to 44 institutions, with a median of $336K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 82% were R1s, 14% R2s and 2% RCUs.
