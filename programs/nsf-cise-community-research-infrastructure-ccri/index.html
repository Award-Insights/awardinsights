CISE Community Research Infrastructure (CCRI)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 735900 · Computer & Info Sci

NSF made $82M in awards (full value) under this program to 93 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 74 awards to 52 institutions, with a median of $330K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 88% were R1s, 8% R2s and 2% RCUs.
