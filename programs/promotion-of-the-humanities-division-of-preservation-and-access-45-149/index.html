Promotion of the Humanities Division of Preservation and Access

National Endowment for the Humanities (NEH) · Assistance Listing 45.149

NEH obligated $55M under this program to 177 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 115 new grants and cooperative agreements to 87 institutions, with a median of $249K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 60% were R1s, 16% R2s and 7% RCUs.
