Workforce Program in the Mathematical Sciences

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 733500 · Math & Physical Sci

NSF made $24M in awards (full value) under this program to 30 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 13 awards to 13 institutions, with a median of $2.5M (FY2026 still being revised).
