Grants for Agricultural Research, Special Research Grants

Agriculture (USDA) · Assistance Listing 10.200

USDA obligated $134M under this program to 36 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 40 new grants and cooperative agreements to 25 institutions, with a median of $448K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 88% were R1s and 12% R2s.
