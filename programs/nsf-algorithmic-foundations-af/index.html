Algorithmic Foundations (AF)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 779600 · Computer & Info Sci

NSF made $132M in awards (full value) under this program to 106 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 176 awards to 80 institutions, with a median of $449K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 80% were R1s, 11% R2s and 8% RCUs.
