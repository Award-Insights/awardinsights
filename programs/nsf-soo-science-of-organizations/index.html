SoO-Science Of Organizations

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 803100 · Social/Behav/Econ

NSF made $14M in awards (full value) under this program to 47 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 39 awards to 32 institutions, with a median of $300K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 94% were R1s and 6% R2s.
