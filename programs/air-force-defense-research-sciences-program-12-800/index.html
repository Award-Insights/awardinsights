Air Force Defense Research Sciences Program

Defense (DOD) · Assistance Listing 12.800

DOD obligated $2.19B under this program to 292 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 1,034 new grants and cooperative agreements to 219 institutions, with a median of $150K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 81% were R1s, 15% R2s and 3% RCUs.
