National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 152100 · Geosciences

NSF made $45M in awards (full value) under this program to 50 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 82 awards to 35 institutions, with a median of $294K (FY2026 still being revised).
