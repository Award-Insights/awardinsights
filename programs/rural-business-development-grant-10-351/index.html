Rural Business Development Grant

Agriculture (USDA) · Assistance Listing 10.351

USDA obligated $23M under this program to 103 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 106 new grants and cooperative agreements to 64 institutions, with a median of $95K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 31% were R1s, 7% R2s and 10% RCUs.
