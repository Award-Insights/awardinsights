Chemical Structure and Dynamics (CSD)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 910100 · Math & Physical Sci

NSF made $89M in awards (full value) under this program to 109 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 123 awards to 74 institutions, with a median of $525K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 86% were R1s, 5% R2s and 4% RCUs.
