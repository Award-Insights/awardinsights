Space Technology

NASA (NASA) · Assistance Listing 43.012

NASA obligated $285M under this program to 136 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 260 new grants and cooperative agreements to 81 institutions, with a median of $84K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 91% were R1s, 7% R2s and 1% RCUs.
