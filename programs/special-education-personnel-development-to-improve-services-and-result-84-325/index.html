Education (ED) · Assistance Listing 84.325

ED obligated $490M under this program to 215 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 194 new grants and cooperative agreements to 129 institutions, with a median of $244K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 47% were R1s, 20% R2s and 13% RCUs.
