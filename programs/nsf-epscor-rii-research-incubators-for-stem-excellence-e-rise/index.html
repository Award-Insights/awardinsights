EPSCoR RII: Research Incubators for STEM Excellence (E-RISE)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 269Y00 · Office of Director

NSF made $140M in awards (full value) under this program to 40 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 46 awards to 40 institutions, with a median of $1.5M (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 40% were R1s, 18% R2s and 25% RCUs.
