Crop Protection and Pest Management Competitive Grants Program

Agriculture (USDA) · Assistance Listing 10.329

USDA obligated $98M under this program to 59 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 106 new grants and cooperative agreements to 55 institutions, with a median of $241K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 91% were R1s, 7% R2s and 2% RCUs.
