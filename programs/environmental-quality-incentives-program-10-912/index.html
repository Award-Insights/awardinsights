Environmental Quality Incentives Program

Agriculture (USDA) · Assistance Listing 10.912

USDA obligated $117M under this program to 68 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 69 new grants and cooperative agreements to 41 institutions, with a median of $553K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 73% were R1s, 15% R2s and 7% RCUs.
