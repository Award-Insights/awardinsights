Education and Human Resources

National Science Foundation (NSF) · Assistance Listing 47.076

NSF obligated $5.96B under this program to 1,096 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 2,708 new grants and cooperative agreements to 798 institutions, with a median of $400K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 23% were R1s, 13% R2s and 13% RCUs.
