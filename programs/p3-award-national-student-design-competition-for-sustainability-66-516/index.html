P3 Award: National Student Design Competition for Sustainability

Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) · Assistance Listing 66.516

EPA obligated $3.7M under this program to 56 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 50 new grants and cooperative agreements to 44 institutions, with a median of $75K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 64% were R1s, 18% R2s and 9% RCUs.
