HBCU-EiR Co-Funding

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 259Y00 · Math & Physical Sci

NSF made $11M in awards (full value) under this program to 16 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 17 awards to 16 institutions, with a median of $400K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 12% were R1s, 19% R2s and 31% RCUs.
