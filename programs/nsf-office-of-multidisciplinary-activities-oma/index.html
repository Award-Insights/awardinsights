Office of Multidisciplinary Activities (OMA)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 125300 · Math & Physical Sci

NSF made $473M in awards (full value) under this program to 296 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 640 awards to 217 institutions, with a median of $411K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 62% were R1s, 18% R2s and 11% RCUs.
