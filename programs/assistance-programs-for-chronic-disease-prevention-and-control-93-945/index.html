Assistance Programs for Chronic Disease Prevention and Control

Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) · Assistance Listing 93.945

CDC obligated $109M under this program to 32 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2022–24, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 34 new grants and cooperative agreements to 26 institutions, with a median of $1000K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 77% were R1s, 12% R2s and 8% RCUs.
