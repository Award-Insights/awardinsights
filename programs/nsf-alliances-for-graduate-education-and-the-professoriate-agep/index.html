Alliances for Graduate Education and the Professoriate (AGEP)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 151500 · STEM Education

NSF made $23M in awards (full value) under this program to 31 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 22 awards to 20 institutions, with a median of $450K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 95% were R1s and 5% R2s.
