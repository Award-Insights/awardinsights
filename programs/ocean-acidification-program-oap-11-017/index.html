Ocean Acidification Program (OAP)

Commerce (DOC) · Assistance Listing 11.017

DOC obligated $27M under this program to 28 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 33 new grants and cooperative agreements to 23 institutions, with a median of $599K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 81% were R1s, 5% R2s and 10% RCUs.
