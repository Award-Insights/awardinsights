Human-Centered Computing (HCC)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 736700 · Computer & Info Sci

NSF made $215M in awards (full value) under this program to 155 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 297 awards to 121 institutions, with a median of $500K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 74% were R1s, 17% R2s and 6% RCUs.
