Aging Research

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · Assistance Listing 93.866

NIH obligated $15.60B under this program to 312 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 2,856 new grants and cooperative agreements to 233 institutions, with a median of $440K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 72% were R1s, 17% R2s and 10% RCUs.
