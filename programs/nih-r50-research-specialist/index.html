NIH R50: Research specialist

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code R50, all institutes

NIH awarded $77M in total cost under activity code R50 to 62 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 102 new and renewal awards to 48 institutions, with a median of $161K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 91% were R1s, 7% R2s and 2% RCUs.
