Cellular Dynamics and Function (CDF)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 111400 · Bio Sciences

NSF made $133M in awards (full value) under this program to 118 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 99 awards to 74 institutions, with a median of $827K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 85% were R1s, 7% R2s and 7% RCUs.
