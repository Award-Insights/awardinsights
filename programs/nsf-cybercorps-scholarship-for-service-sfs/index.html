CyberCorps: Scholarship for Service (SFS)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 166800 · STEM Education

NSF made $247M in awards (full value) under this program to 100 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 81 awards to 64 institutions, with a median of $970K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 77% were R1s, 16% R2s and 6% RCUs.
