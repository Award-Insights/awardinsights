Promotion of the Arts Grants to Organizations and Individuals

National Endowment for the Arts (NEA) · Assistance Listing 45.024

NEA obligated $29M under this program to 292 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 456 new grants and cooperative agreements to 206 institutions, with a median of $30K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 49% were R1s, 13% R2s and 11% RCUs.
