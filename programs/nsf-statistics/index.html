Statistics

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 126900 · Math & Physical Sci

NSF made $93M in awards (full value) under this program to 125 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 321 awards to 102 institutions, with a median of $175K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 92% were R1s, 5% R2s and 3% RCUs.
