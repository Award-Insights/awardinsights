Chemical Catalysis (CAT)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 688400 · Math & Physical Sci

NSF made $131M in awards (full value) under this program to 136 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 173 awards to 98 institutions, with a median of $537K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 88% were R1s, 6% R2s and 5% RCUs.
