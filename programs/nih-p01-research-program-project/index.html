NIH P01: Research program project

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code P01, all institutes

NIH awarded $3.03B in total cost under activity code P01 to 103 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 144 new and renewal awards to 71 institutions, with a median of $2.5M (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 87% were R1s, 10% R2s and 1% RCUs.
