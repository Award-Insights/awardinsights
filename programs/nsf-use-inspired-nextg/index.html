National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 350Y00 · Computer & Info Sci

NSF made $23M in awards (full value) under this program to 44 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 52 awards to 44 institutions, with a median of $483K (FY2026 still being revised).
