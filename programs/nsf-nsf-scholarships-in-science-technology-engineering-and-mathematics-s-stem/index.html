NSF Scholarships in Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics (S-STEM)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 153600 · STEM Education

NSF made $614M in awards (full value) under this program to 327 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 185 awards to 167 institutions, with a median of $2.0M (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 34% were R1s, 16% R2s and 11% RCUs.
