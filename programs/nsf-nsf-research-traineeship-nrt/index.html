NSF Research Traineeship (NRT)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 199700 · STEM Education

NSF made $235M in awards (full value) under this program to 101 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 56 awards to 54 institutions, with a median of $3.0M (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 74% were R1s, 17% R2s and 6% RCUs.
