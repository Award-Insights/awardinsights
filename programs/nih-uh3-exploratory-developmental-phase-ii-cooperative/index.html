NIH UH3: Exploratory/developmental, phase II, cooperative

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code UH3, all institutes

NIH awarded $1.40B in total cost under activity code UH3 to 113 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 11 new and renewal awards to 10 institutions, with a median of $558K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 80% were R1s and 10% R2s.
