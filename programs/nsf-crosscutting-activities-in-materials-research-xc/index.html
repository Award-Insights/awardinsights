Crosscutting Activities in Materials Research (XC)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 722200 · Geosciences

NSF made $107M in awards (full value) under this program to 193 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 184 awards to 137 institutions, with a median of $357K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 66% were R1s, 12% R2s and 12% RCUs.
