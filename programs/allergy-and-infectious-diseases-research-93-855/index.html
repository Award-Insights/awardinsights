Allergy and Infectious Diseases Research

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · Assistance Listing 93.855

NIH obligated $16.45B under this program to 367 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 5,058 new grants and cooperative agreements to 291 institutions, with a median of $273K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 63% were R1s, 21% R2s and 14% RCUs.
