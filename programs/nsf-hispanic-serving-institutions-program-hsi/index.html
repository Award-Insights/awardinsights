Hispanic-Serving Institutions Program (HSI)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 077Y00 · STEM Education

NSF made $184M in awards (full value) under this program to 151 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 163 awards to 104 institutions, with a median of $271K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 24% were R1s, 15% R2s and 14% RCUs.
