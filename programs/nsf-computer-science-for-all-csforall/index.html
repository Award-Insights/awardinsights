Computer Science for All (CSforAll)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 134Y00 · Computer & Info Sci

NSF made $68M in awards (full value) under this program to 85 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 48 awards to 41 institutions, with a median of $445K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 66% were R1s, 17% R2s and 12% RCUs.
