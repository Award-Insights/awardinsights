Special Initiatives

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 164200 · Engineering

NSF made $173M in awards (full value) under this program to 196 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 313 awards to 166 institutions, with a median of $336K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 68% were R1s, 21% R2s and 7% RCUs.
