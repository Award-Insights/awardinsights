Quantum Information Science, Engineering, and Technology (QISET)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 105Y00 · Math & Physical Sci

NSF made $271M in awards (full value) under this program to 62 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 83 awards to 48 institutions, with a median of $500K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 92% were R1s, 6% R2s and 2% RCUs.
