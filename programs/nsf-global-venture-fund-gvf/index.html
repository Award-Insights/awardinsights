Global Venture Fund (GVF)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 054Y00 · Math & Physical Sci

NSF made $139M in awards (full value) under this program to 111 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 148 awards to 86 institutions, with a median of $420K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 87% were R1s, 8% R2s and 5% RCUs.
