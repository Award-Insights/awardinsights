International Research and Research Training

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · Assistance Listing 93.989

NIH obligated $319M under this program to 98 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 120 new grants and cooperative agreements to 65 institutions, with a median of $170K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 94% were R1s and 6% R2s.
