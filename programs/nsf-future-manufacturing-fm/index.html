Future Manufacturing (FM)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 142Y00 · Engineering

NSF made $70M in awards (full value) under this program to 45 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 22 awards to 18 institutions, with a median of $500K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 83% were R1s and 17% R2s.
