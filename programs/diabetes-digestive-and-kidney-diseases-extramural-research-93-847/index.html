Diabetes, Digestive, and Kidney Diseases Extramural Research

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · Assistance Listing 93.847

NIH obligated $7.85B under this program to 244 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 2,011 new grants and cooperative agreements to 179 institutions, with a median of $465K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 74% were R1s, 17% R2s and 6% RCUs.
