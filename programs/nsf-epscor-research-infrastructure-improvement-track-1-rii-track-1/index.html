EPSCoR Research Infrastructure Improvement Track-1 (RII Track-1)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 193Y00 · Office of Director

NSF made $211M in awards (full value) under this program to 15 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2022–24, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 21 awards to 15 institutions, with a median of $20M. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 80% were R1s, 13% R2s and 7% RCUs.
