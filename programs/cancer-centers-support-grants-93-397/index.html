Cancer Centers Support Grants

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · Assistance Listing 93.397

NIH obligated $2.01B under this program to 92 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 19 new grants and cooperative agreements to 15 institutions, with a median of $2.2M (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 93% were R1s and 7% RCUs.
