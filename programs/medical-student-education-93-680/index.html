Medical Student Education

Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA) · Assistance Listing 93.680

HRSA obligated $296M under this program to 18 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 19 new grants and cooperative agreements to 18 institutions, with a median of $4.0M. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 67% were R1s and 33% R2s.
