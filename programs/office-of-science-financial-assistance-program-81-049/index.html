Office of Science Financial Assistance Program

Energy (DOE) · Assistance Listing 81.049

DOE obligated $5.49B under this program to 341 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 1,231 new grants and cooperative agreements to 234 institutions, with a median of $640K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 67% were R1s, 19% R2s and 10% RCUs.
