Research and Training in Complementary and Integrative Health

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · Assistance Listing 93.213

NIH obligated $451M under this program to 125 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 191 new grants and cooperative agreements to 84 institutions, with a median of $432K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 83% were R1s, 10% R2s and 5% RCUs.
