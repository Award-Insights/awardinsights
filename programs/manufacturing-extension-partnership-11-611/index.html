Commerce (DOC) · Assistance Listing 11.611

DOC obligated $186M under this program to 22 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 15 new grants and cooperative agreements to 15 institutions, with a median of $2.4M (FY2026 still being revised).
