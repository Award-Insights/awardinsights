Advanced Cyberinfrastructure Core (OAC Core)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 090Y00 · Computer & Info Sci

NSF made $27M in awards (full value) under this program to 51 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 42 awards to 31 institutions, with a median of $300K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 94% were R1s and 6% R2s.
