International Research Experiences for Students (IRES)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 288Y00 · Office of Director

NSF made $31M in awards (full value) under this program to 58 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 69 awards to 58 institutions, with a median of $450K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 74% were R1s, 16% R2s and 3% RCUs.
