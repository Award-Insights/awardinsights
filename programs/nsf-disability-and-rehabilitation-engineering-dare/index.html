Disability and Rehabilitation Engineering (DARE)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 534200 · Engineering

NSF made $33M in awards (full value) under this program to 83 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 62 awards to 48 institutions, with a median of $457K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 75% were R1s, 19% R2s and 2% RCUs.
