Second Chance Act Reentry Initiative

Justice (DOJ) · Assistance Listing 16.812

DOJ obligated $11M under this program to 13 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 12 new grants and cooperative agreements to 12 institutions, with a median of $900K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 42% were R1s, 8% R2s and 8% RCUs.
