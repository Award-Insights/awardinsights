Advanced Research Projects Agency for Health (ARPA-H)

Advanced Research Projects Agency for Health (ARPA-H) · Assistance Listing 93.384

ARPA-H obligated $1.58B under this program to 61 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 128 new grants and cooperative agreements to 60 institutions, with a median of $6.5M (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 89% were R1s, 9% R2s and 2% RCUs.
