Motor Carrier Safety Assistance High Priority Activities Grants and Cooperative Agreements

Transportation (DOT) · Assistance Listing 20.237

DOT obligated $56M under this program to 21 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 31 new grants and cooperative agreements to 17 institutions, with a median of $897K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 88% were R1s and 12% R2s.
