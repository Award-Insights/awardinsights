NIH RF1: Multi-year funded research project

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code RF1, all institutes

NIH awarded $2.00B in total cost under activity code RF1 to 151 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 259 new and renewal awards to 91 institutions, with a median of $2.5M (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 84% were R1s, 10% R2s and 3% RCUs.
