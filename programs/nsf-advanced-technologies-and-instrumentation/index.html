Advanced Technologies and Instrumentation

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 121800 · Math & Physical Sci

NSF made $30M in awards (full value) under this program to 35 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 34 awards to 25 institutions, with a median of $570K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 88% were R1s and 8% R2s.
