Experiential Learning for Emerging and Novel Technologies (ExLENT)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 227Y00 · Tech, Innov & Partnerships

NSF made $60M in awards (full value) under this program to 87 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 73 awards to 69 institutions, with a median of $990K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 61% were R1s, 12% R2s and 9% RCUs.
