NIH U2R: International training, cooperative

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code U2R, all institutes

NIH awarded $22M in total cost under activity code U2R to 17 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2020–22, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 7 new and renewal awards to 7 institutions, with a median of $293K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 86% were R1s and 14% R2s.
