Financial Assistance for Targeted Violence and Terrorism Prevention

Homeland Security (DHS) · Assistance Listing 97.132

DHS obligated $23M under this program to 38 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 31 new grants and cooperative agreements to 29 institutions, with a median of $516K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 62% were R1s, 15% R2s and 12% RCUs.
