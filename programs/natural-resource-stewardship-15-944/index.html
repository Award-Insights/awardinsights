Natural Resource Stewardship

Interior (DOI) · Assistance Listing 15.944

DOI obligated $6.4M under this program to 21 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 9 new grants and cooperative agreements to 7 institutions, with a median of $192K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 57% were R1s, 14% R2s and 29% RCUs.
