NIH R25: Education project

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code R25, all institutes

NIH awarded $851M in total cost under activity code R25 to 264 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 415 new and renewal awards to 146 institutions, with a median of $246K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 69% were R1s, 21% R2s and 8% RCUs.
