Social, Behavioral, and Economic Sciences

National Science Foundation (NSF) · Assistance Listing 47.075

NSF obligated $923M under this program to 335 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 1,411 new grants and cooperative agreements to 261 institutions, with a median of $150K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 61% were R1s, 17% R2s and 14% RCUs.
