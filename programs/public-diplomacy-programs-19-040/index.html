Public Diplomacy Programs

State (State) · Assistance Listing 19.040

State obligated $30M under this program to 135 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 108 new grants and cooperative agreements to 65 institutions, with a median of $62K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 75% were R1s, 10% R2s and 4% RCUs.
