NIH U42: Animal and tissue resources, cooperative

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code U42, all institutes

NIH awarded $158M in total cost under activity code U42 to 11 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 14 new and renewal awards to 9 institutions, with a median of $1.5M (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 78% were R1s, 11% R2s and 11% RCUs.
