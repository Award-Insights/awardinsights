Grants to Reduce Domestic Violence, Dating Violence, Sexual Assault, and Stalking on Campus

Justice (DOJ) · Assistance Listing 16.525

DOJ obligated $53M under this program to 136 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2022–24, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 137 new grants and cooperative agreements to 136 institutions, with a median of $400K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 6% were R1s, 14% R2s and 8% RCUs.
