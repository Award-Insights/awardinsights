Cooperative Research and Training Programs – Resources of the National Park System

Interior (DOI) · Assistance Listing 15.945

DOI obligated $253M under this program to 195 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 635 new grants and cooperative agreements to 158 institutions, with a median of $81K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 63% were R1s, 14% R2s and 14% RCUs.
