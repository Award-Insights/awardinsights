Promotion of the Humanities Teaching and Learning Resources and Curriculum Development

National Endowment for the Humanities (NEH) · Assistance Listing 45.162

NEH obligated $35M under this program to 253 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 122 new grants and cooperative agreements to 115 institutions, with a median of $110K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 25% were R1s, 13% R2s and 13% RCUs.
