Educational Linkages

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 770000 · Geosciences

NSF made $24M in awards (full value) under this program to 39 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 28 awards to 25 institutions, with a median of $516K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 68% were R1s, 24% R2s and 4% RCUs.
