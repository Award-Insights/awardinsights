Office of International Science and Engineering

National Science Foundation (NSF) · Assistance Listing 47.079

NSF obligated $249M under this program to 192 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 284 new grants and cooperative agreements to 136 institutions, with a median of $250K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 79% were R1s, 11% R2s and 6% RCUs.
