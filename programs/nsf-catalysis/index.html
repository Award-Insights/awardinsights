Catalysis

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 140100 · Engineering

NSF made $49M in awards (full value) under this program to 80 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 89 awards to 55 institutions, with a median of $371K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 85% were R1s and 13% R2s.
