Launching Early-Career Academic Pathways in the Mathematical and Physical Sciences (LEAPS-MPS)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 217Y00 · Math & Physical Sci

NSF made $28M in awards (full value) under this program to 98 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 70 awards to 61 institutions, with a median of $250K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 31% were R1s, 26% R2s and 23% RCUs.
