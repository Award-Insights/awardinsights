NIH T35: Short-term research training

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code T35, all institutes

NIH awarded $36M in total cost under activity code T35 to 69 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 42 new and renewal awards to 35 institutions, with a median of $89K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 83% were R1s, 9% R2s and 6% RCUs.
