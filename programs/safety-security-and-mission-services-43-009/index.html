NASA (NASA) · Assistance Listing 43.009

NASA obligated $41M under this program to 50 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 36 new grants and cooperative agreements to 32 institutions, with a median of $71K (FY2026 still being revised).
