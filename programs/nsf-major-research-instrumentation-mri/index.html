Major Research Instrumentation (MRI)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 118900 · Math & Physical Sci

NSF made $269M in awards (full value) under this program to 273 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 179 awards to 148 institutions, with a median of $634K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 53% were R1s, 22% R2s and 14% RCUs.
