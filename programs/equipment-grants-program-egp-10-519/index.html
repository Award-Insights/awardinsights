Equipment Grants Program (EGP)

Agriculture (USDA) · Assistance Listing 10.519

USDA obligated $21M under this program to 59 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 38 new grants and cooperative agreements to 36 institutions, with a median of $359K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 79% were R1s, 18% R2s and 3% RCUs.
