21st Century Cures Act - Beau Biden Cancer Moonshot

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · Assistance Listing 93.353

NIH obligated $359M under this program to 58 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 35 new grants and cooperative agreements to 27 institutions, with a median of $590K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 89% were R1s, 7% R2s and 4% RCUs.
