Basic Scientific Research

Defense (DOD) · Assistance Listing 12.431

DOD obligated $2.26B under this program to 278 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 1,078 new grants and cooperative agreements to 208 institutions, with a median of $140K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 77% were R1s, 16% R2s and 5% RCUs.
