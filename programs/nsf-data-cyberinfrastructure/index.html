Data Cyberinfrastructure

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 772600 · Computer & Info Sci

NSF made $45M in awards (full value) under this program to 28 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 24 awards to 22 institutions, with a median of $392K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 95% were R1s and 5% R2s.
