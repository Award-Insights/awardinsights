National Historical Publications and Records Grants

National Archives and Records Administration (NARA) · Assistance Listing 89.003

NARA obligated $37M under this program to 62 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 46 new grants and cooperative agreements to 36 institutions, with a median of $125K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 67% were R1s, 14% R2s and 6% RCUs.
