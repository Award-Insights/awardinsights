ReDDDoT-Resp Des Development & Dp Technology

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 293Y00 · Tech, Innov & Partnerships

NSF made $9.8M in awards (full value) under this program to 27 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2022–24, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 29 awards to 27 institutions, with a median of $300K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 85% were R1s and 11% R2s.
