Ryan White HIV/AIDS Dental Reimbursement and Community Based Dental Partnership Grants

Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA) · Assistance Listing 93.924

HRSA obligated $39M under this program to 36 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 69 new grants and cooperative agreements to 27 institutions, with a median of $66K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 67% were R1s, 11% R2s and 11% RCUs.
