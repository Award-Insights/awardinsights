Interior (DOI) · Assistance Listing 15.810

DOI obligated $38M under this program to 80 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 96 new grants and cooperative agreements to 54 institutions, with a median of $87K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 65% were R1s, 15% R2s and 15% RCUs.
