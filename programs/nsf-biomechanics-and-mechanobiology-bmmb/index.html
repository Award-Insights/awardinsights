Biomechanics and Mechanobiology (BMMB)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 747900 · Engineering

NSF made $65M in awards (full value) under this program to 113 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 116 awards to 78 institutions, with a median of $407K (FY2026 still being revised).
