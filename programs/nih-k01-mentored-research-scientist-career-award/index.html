NIH K01: Mentored research scientist career award

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code K01, all institutes

NIH awarded $725M in total cost under activity code K01 to 204 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 490 new and renewal awards to 116 institutions, with a median of $161K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 86% were R1s, 12% R2s and 2% RCUs.
