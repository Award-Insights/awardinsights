NIH U2C: Multi-component resource, cooperative

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code U2C, all institutes

NIH awarded $455M in total cost under activity code U2C to 42 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 16 new and renewal awards to 15 institutions, with a median of $368K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 87% were R1s and 13% R2s.
