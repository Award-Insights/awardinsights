NIH U19: Research program, cooperative

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code U19, all institutes

NIH awarded $3.38B in total cost under activity code U19 to 80 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 62 new and renewal awards to 43 institutions, with a median of $3.9M (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 89% were R1s and 11% R2s.
