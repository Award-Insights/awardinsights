SBP-Science of Broadening Part

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 110Y00 · Social/Behav/Econ

NSF made $10M in awards (full value) under this program to 53 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 41 awards to 36 institutions, with a median of $365K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 75% were R1s, 14% R2s and 8% RCUs.
