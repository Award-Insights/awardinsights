Office of STEM Engagement (OSTEM)

NASA (NASA) · Assistance Listing 43.008

NASA obligated $465M under this program to 166 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 196 new grants and cooperative agreements to 101 institutions, with a median of $400K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 57% were R1s, 24% R2s and 10% RCUs.
