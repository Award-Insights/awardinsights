Primary Care Training and Enhancement

Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA) · Assistance Listing 93.884

HRSA obligated $193M under this program to 85 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 43 new grants and cooperative agreements to 41 institutions, with a median of $498K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 68% were R1s, 15% R2s and 12% RCUs.
