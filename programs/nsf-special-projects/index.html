Special Projects

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 226Y00 · Tech, Innov & Partnerships

NSF made $154M in awards (full value) under this program to 71 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 68 awards to 49 institutions, with a median of $300K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 94% were R1s, 2% R2s and 4% RCUs.
