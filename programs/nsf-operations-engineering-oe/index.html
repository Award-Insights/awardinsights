Operations Engineering (OE)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 006Y00 · Engineering

NSF made $32M in awards (full value) under this program to 68 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 70 awards to 51 institutions, with a median of $348K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 78% were R1s, 18% R2s and 4% RCUs.
