Higher Education - Institution Challenge Grants Program

Agriculture (USDA) · Assistance Listing 10.217

USDA obligated $15M under this program to 39 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 32 new grants and cooperative agreements to 26 institutions, with a median of $149K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 85% were R1s and 15% R2s.
