Birth Defects and Developmental Disabilities - Prevention and Surveillance

Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) · Assistance Listing 93.073

CDC obligated $67M under this program to 25 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2021–23, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 23 new grants and cooperative agreements to 19 institutions, with a median of $415K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 89% were R1s, 5% R2s and 5% RCUs.
