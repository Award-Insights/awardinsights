Americorps Seniors Retired and Senior Volunteer Program (RSVP) 94.002

AmeriCorps (Corporation for National and Community Service) (AmeriCorps) · Assistance Listing 94.002

AmeriCorps obligated $14M under this program to 37 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 29 new grants and cooperative agreements to 27 institutions, with a median of $92K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 19% were R1s, 11% R2s and 11% RCUs.
