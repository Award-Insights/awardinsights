Improving Undergraduate STEM Education (IUSE)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 199800 · STEM Education

NSF made $451M in awards (full value) under this program to 409 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 634 awards to 280 institutions, with a median of $370K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 47% were R1s, 19% R2s and 11% RCUs.
