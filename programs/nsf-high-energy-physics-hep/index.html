High Energy Physics (HEP)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 122100 · Math & Physical Sci

NSF made $63M in awards (full value) under this program to 34 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 40 awards to 28 institutions, with a median of $540K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 71% were R1s, 18% R2s and 7% RCUs.
