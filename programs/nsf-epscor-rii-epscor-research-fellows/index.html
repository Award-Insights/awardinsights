EPSCoR RII: EPSCoR Research Fellows

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 196Y00 · Office of Director

NSF made $73M in awards (full value) under this program to 73 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 211 awards to 66 institutions, with a median of $292K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 59% were R1s, 18% R2s and 18% RCUs.
