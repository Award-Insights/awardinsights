NIH K22: Career transition award

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code K22, all institutes

NIH awarded $69M in total cost under activity code K22 to 101 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 85 new and renewal awards to 55 institutions, with a median of $187K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 87% were R1s, 9% R2s and 2% RCUs.
