Child Health and Human Development Extramural Research

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · Assistance Listing 93.865

NIH obligated $4.81B under this program to 290 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 1,954 new grants and cooperative agreements to 207 institutions, with a median of $351K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 75% were R1s, 15% R2s and 7% RCUs.
