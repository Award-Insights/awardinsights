Human-Environment and Geographical Sciences (DDRI)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 160Y00 · Social/Behav/Econ

NSF made $596K in awards (full value) under this program to 19 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 22 awards to 15 institutions, with a median of $21K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 93% were R1s and 7% R2s.
