Cybersecurity Innovation for Cyberinfrastructure (CICI)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 802700 · Computer & Info Sci

NSF made $73M in awards (full value) under this program to 53 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 57 awards to 40 institutions, with a median of $600K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 92% were R1s and 5% R2s.
