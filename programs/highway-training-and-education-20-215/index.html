Highway Training and Education

Transportation (DOT) · Assistance Listing 20.215

DOT obligated $22M under this program to 121 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 436 new grants and cooperative agreements to 90 institutions, with a median of $6K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 64% were R1s, 22% R2s and 2% RCUs.
