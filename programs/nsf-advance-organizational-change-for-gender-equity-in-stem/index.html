ADVANCE: Organizational Change for Gender Equity in STEM

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 016Y00 · STEM Education

NSF made $55M in awards (full value) under this program to 82 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 54 awards to 51 institutions, with a median of $401K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 63% were R1s, 20% R2s and 8% RCUs.
