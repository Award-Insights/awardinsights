NIH R35: Outstanding investigator (including MIRA)

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code R35, all institutes

NIH awarded $7.67B in total cost under activity code R35 to 246 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 2,347 new and renewal awards to 215 institutions, with a median of $428K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 79% were R1s, 15% R2s and 5% RCUs.
