Drug Abuse and Addiction Research Programs

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · Assistance Listing 93.279

NIH obligated $5.89B under this program to 262 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 1,520 new grants and cooperative agreements to 189 institutions, with a median of $418K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 75% were R1s, 16% R2s and 6% RCUs.
