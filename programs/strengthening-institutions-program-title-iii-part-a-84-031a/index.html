Strengthening Institutions Program (Title III, Part A)

Education (ED) · Assistance Listing 84.031A
One competition of Higher Education Institutional Aid (84.031), told apart by the letter in Education's award numbers.

ED obligated $523M under this program to 393 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 62 new grants and cooperative agreements to 62 institutions, with a median of $238K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 5% were R1s, 5% R2s and 2% RCUs.
