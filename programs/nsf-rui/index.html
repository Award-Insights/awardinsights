RUI: Research in Undergraduate Institutions

National Science Foundation (NSF) · across NSF's directorates
Found by NSF's own naming: awards titled “RUI:”, also after “Collaborative Research:”; every partner on a collaborative project carries the title, so some recipients are partners rather than the undergraduate institution.

NSF made $218M in awards (full value) under this program to 291 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 324 awards to 197 institutions, with a median of $300K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 23% were R1s, 13% R2s and 31% RCUs.
