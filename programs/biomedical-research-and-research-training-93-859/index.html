Biomedical Research and Research Training

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · Assistance Listing 93.859

NIH obligated $13.39B under this program to 504 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 3,231 new grants and cooperative agreements to 377 institutions, with a median of $393K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 49% were R1s, 24% R2s and 15% RCUs.
