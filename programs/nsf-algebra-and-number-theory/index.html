National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 126400 · Math & Physical Sci

NSF made $109M in awards (full value) under this program to 140 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 332 awards to 120 institutions, with a median of $177K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 82% were R1s, 6% R2s and 8% RCUs.
