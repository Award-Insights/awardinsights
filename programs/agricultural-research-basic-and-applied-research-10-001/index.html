Agricultural Research Basic and Applied Research

Agriculture (USDA) · Assistance Listing 10.001

USDA obligated $376M under this program to 138 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 347 new grants and cooperative agreements to 73 institutions, with a median of $100K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 79% were R1s, 11% R2s and 7% RCUs.
