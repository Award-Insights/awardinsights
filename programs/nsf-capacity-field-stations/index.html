NSF made $6.9M in awards (full value) under this program to 22 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 16 awards to 16 institutions, with a median of $197K (FY2026 still being revised).
