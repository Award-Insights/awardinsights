Training in General, Pediatric, and Public Health Dentistry

Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA) · Assistance Listing 93.059

HRSA obligated $86M under this program to 41 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 15 new grants and cooperative agreements to 13 institutions, with a median of $113K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 69% were R1s, 15% R2s and 8% RCUs.
