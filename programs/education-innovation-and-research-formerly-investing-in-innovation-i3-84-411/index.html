Education Innovation and Research (formerly Investing in Innovation (I3) Fund)

Education (ED) · Assistance Listing 84.411

ED obligated $407M under this program to 43 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 31 new grants and cooperative agreements to 26 institutions, with a median of $8.0M (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 73% were R1s, 15% R2s and 8% RCUs.
