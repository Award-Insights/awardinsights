Software and Hardware Foundations (SHF)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 779800 · Computer & Info Sci

NSF made $269M in awards (full value) under this program to 163 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 359 awards to 128 institutions, with a median of $498K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 86% were R1s, 10% R2s and 2% RCUs.
