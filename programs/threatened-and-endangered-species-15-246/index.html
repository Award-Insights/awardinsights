Threatened and Endangered Species

Interior (DOI) · Assistance Listing 15.246

DOI obligated $6.4M under this program to 29 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 26 new grants and cooperative agreements to 17 institutions, with a median of $75K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 71% were R1s, 12% R2s and 12% RCUs.
