Fish and Wildlife Coordination Act

Interior (DOI) · Assistance Listing 15.517

DOI obligated $9.7M under this program to 19 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 17 new grants and cooperative agreements to 11 institutions, with a median of $177K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 82% were R1s, 9% R2s and 9% RCUs.
