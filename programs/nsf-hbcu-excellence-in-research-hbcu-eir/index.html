HBCU Excellence in Research (HBCU-EiR)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 070Y00 · Engineering

NSF made $111M in awards (full value) under this program to 46 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 138 awards to 42 institutions, with a median of $596K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 5% were R1s, 29% R2s and 48% RCUs.
