Biological Sciences

National Science Foundation (NSF) · Assistance Listing 47.074

NSF obligated $3.21B under this program to 520 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 2,143 new grants and cooperative agreements to 393 institutions, with a median of $465K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 46% were R1s, 19% R2s and 15% RCUs.
