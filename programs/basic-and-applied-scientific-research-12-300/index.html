Basic and Applied Scientific Research

Interior (DOI) · Assistance Listing 12.300

DOI obligated $3.36B under this program to 315 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 1,718 new grants and cooperative agreements to 242 institutions, with a median of $100K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 77% were R1s, 15% R2s and 5% RCUs.
