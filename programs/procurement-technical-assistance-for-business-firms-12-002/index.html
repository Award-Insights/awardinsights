Procurement Technical Assistance for Business Firms

Defense (DOD) · Assistance Listing 12.002

DOD obligated $119M under this program to 43 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 125 new grants and cooperative agreements to 43 institutions, with a median of $316K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 46% were R1s, 15% R2s and 12% RCUs.
