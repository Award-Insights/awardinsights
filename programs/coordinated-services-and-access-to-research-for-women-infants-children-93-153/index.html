Coordinated Services and Access to Research for Women, Infants, Children, and Youth

Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA) · Assistance Listing 93.153

HRSA obligated $117M under this program to 38 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 35 new grants and cooperative agreements to 35 institutions, with a median of $519K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 82% were R1s, 9% R2s and 9% RCUs.
