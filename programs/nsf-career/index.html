CAREER: Faculty Early Career Development

National Science Foundation (NSF) · across NSF's directorates
Found by NSF's own naming: every CAREER award's title begins “CAREER:”.

NSF made $3.09B in awards (full value) under this program to 337 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 2,703 awards to 285 institutions, with a median of $600K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 60% were R1s, 21% R2s and 12% RCUs.
