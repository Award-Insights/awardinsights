Head Start

HHS: other divisions (HHS other) · Assistance Listing 93.600

HHS other obligated $1.21B under this program to 89 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 67 new grants and cooperative agreements to 58 institutions, with a median of $1.4M (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 41% were R1s, 13% R2s and 7% RCUs.
