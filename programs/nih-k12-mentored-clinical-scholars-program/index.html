NIH K12: Mentored clinical scholars program

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code K12, all institutes

NIH awarded $508M in total cost under activity code K12 to 70 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 88 new and renewal awards to 56 institutions, with a median of $756K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 88% were R1s and 12% R2s.
