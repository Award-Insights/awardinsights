Appalachian Area Development

Appalachian Regional Commission (ARC) · Assistance Listing 23.002

ARC obligated $112M under this program to 107 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 108 new grants and cooperative agreements to 67 institutions, with a median of $300K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 20% were R1s, 9% R2s and 2% RCUs.
