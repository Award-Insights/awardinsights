NIH DP1: Pioneer award

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code DP1, all institutes

NIH awarded $420M in total cost under activity code DP1 to 69 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 68 new and renewal awards to 43 institutions, with a median of $776K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 90% were R1s and 10% R2s.
