Enabling Partnerships to Increase Innovation Capacity (EPIIC)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 285Y00 · Tech, Innov & Partnerships

NSF made $29M in awards (full value) under this program to 70 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 71 awards to 70 institutions, with a median of $400K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 4% were R1s, 6% R2s and 31% RCUs.
