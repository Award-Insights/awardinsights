Health Careers Opportunity Program (HCOP)

Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA) · Assistance Listing 93.822

HRSA obligated $67M under this program to 32 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2021–23, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 13 new grants and cooperative agreements to 13 institutions, with a median of $650K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 46% were R1s, 23% R2s and 15% RCUs.
