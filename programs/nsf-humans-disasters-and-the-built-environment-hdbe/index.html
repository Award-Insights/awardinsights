Humans, Disasters, and the Built Environment (HDBE)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 163800 · Engineering

NSF made $17M in awards (full value) under this program to 67 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 42 awards to 34 institutions, with a median of $186K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 88% were R1s and 12% R2s.
