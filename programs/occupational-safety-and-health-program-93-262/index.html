Occupational Safety and Health Program

Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) · Assistance Listing 93.262

CDC obligated $472M under this program to 97 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 76 new grants and cooperative agreements to 55 institutions, with a median of $219K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 73% were R1s, 13% R2s and 9% RCUs.
