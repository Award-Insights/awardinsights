Promotion of the Humanities Professional Development

National Endowment for the Humanities (NEH) · Assistance Listing 45.163

NEH obligated $17M under this program to 79 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 40 new grants and cooperative agreements to 37 institutions, with a median of $175K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 64% were R1s, 6% R2s and 11% RCUs.
