Aircraft Pilots Workforce Development Grant Program

Transportation (DOT) · Assistance Listing 20.111

DOT obligated $8.1M under this program to 20 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2022–24, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 32 new grants and cooperative agreements to 20 institutions, with a median of $384K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 40% were R1s, 5% R2s and 10% RCUs.
