TIP-Chips KTA-3 Quantum

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 315Y00 · Tech, Innov & Partnerships

NSF made $23M in awards (full value) under this program to 27 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 42 awards to 27 institutions, with a median of $100K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 89% were R1s, 7% R2s and 4% RCUs.
