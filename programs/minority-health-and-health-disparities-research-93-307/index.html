Minority Health and Health Disparities Research

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · Assistance Listing 93.307

NIH obligated $1.66B under this program to 168 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 255 new grants and cooperative agreements to 98 institutions, with a median of $425K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 79% were R1s, 16% R2s and 4% RCUs.
