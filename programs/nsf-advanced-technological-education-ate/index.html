Advanced Technological Education (ATE)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 741200 · STEM Education

NSF made $298M in awards (full value) under this program to 241 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 193 awards to 152 institutions, with a median of $565K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 11% were R1s, 1% R2s and 1% RCUs.
