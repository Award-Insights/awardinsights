Economic Adjustment Assistance

Commerce (DOC) · Assistance Listing 11.307

DOC obligated $771M under this program to 169 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 75 new grants and cooperative agreements to 58 institutions, with a median of $1.5M (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 19% were R1s, 17% R2s and 13% RCUs.
