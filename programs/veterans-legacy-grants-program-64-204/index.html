Veterans Legacy Grants Program

Veterans Affairs (VA) · Assistance Listing 64.204

VA obligated $7.0M under this program to 14 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 14 new grants and cooperative agreements to 11 institutions, with a median of $299K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 18% were R1s, 36% R2s and 18% RCUs.
