1890 Institution Capacity Building Grants

Agriculture (USDA) · Assistance Listing 10.216

USDA obligated $118M under this program to 20 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 128 new grants and cooperative agreements to 20 institutions, with a median of $500K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 5% were R1s, 40% R2s and 55% RCUs.
