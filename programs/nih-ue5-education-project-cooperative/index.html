NIH UE5: Education project, cooperative

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code UE5, all institutes

NIH awarded $28M in total cost under activity code UE5 to 45 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 29 new and renewal awards to 28 institutions, with a median of $236K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 79% were R1s, 4% R2s and 7% RCUs.
