Developing Hispanic-Serving Institutions (Title V, Part A)

Education (ED) · Assistance Listing 84.031S
One competition of Higher Education Institutional Aid (84.031), told apart by the letter in Education's award numbers.

ED obligated $622M under this program to 304 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2022–24, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 233 new grants and cooperative agreements to 209 institutions, with a median of $600K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 7% were R1s, 6% R2s and 8% RCUs.
