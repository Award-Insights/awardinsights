Water, Landscape, and Critical Zone (WaLCZ)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 333Y00 · Geosciences

NSF made $37M in awards (full value) under this program to 83 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 110 awards to 78 institutions, with a median of $400K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 74% were R1s, 10% R2s and 10% RCUs.
