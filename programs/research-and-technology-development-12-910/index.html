Research and Technology Development

Interior (DOI) · Assistance Listing 12.910

DOI obligated $1.34B under this program to 137 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 262 new grants and cooperative agreements to 91 institutions, with a median of $250K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 92% were R1s, 7% R2s and 1% RCUs.
