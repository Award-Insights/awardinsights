Smart and Connected Health (SCH)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 801800 · Computer & Info Sci

NSF made $60M in awards (full value) under this program to 84 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 64 awards to 52 institutions, with a median of $499K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 83% were R1s, 13% R2s and 2% RCUs.
