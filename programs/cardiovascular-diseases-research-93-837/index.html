Cardiovascular Diseases Research

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · Assistance Listing 93.837

NIH obligated $11.89B under this program to 292 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 3,505 new grants and cooperative agreements to 229 institutions, with a median of $470K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 66% were R1s, 23% R2s and 9% RCUs.
