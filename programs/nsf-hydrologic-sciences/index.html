Hydrologic Sciences

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 157900 · Geosciences

NSF made $33M in awards (full value) under this program to 86 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 48 awards to 40 institutions, with a median of $374K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 88% were R1s, 8% R2s and 5% RCUs.
