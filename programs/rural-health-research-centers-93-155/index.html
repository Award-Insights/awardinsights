Rural Health Research Centers

Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA) · Assistance Listing 93.155

HRSA obligated $55M under this program to 30 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 18 new grants and cooperative agreements to 18 institutions, with a median of $750K (FY2026 still being revised).
