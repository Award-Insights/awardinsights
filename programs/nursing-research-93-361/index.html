Nursing Research

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · Assistance Listing 93.361

NIH obligated $633M under this program to 114 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 234 new grants and cooperative agreements to 82 institutions, with a median of $428K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 80% were R1s, 15% R2s and 4% RCUs.
