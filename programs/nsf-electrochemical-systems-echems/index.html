Electrochemical Systems (EchemS)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 764400 · Engineering

NSF made $58M in awards (full value) under this program to 102 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 104 awards to 75 institutions, with a median of $396K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 83% were R1s, 15% R2s and 1% RCUs.
