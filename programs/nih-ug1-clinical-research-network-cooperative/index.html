NIH UG1: Clinical research network, cooperative

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code UG1, all institutes

NIH awarded $992M in total cost under activity code UG1 to 78 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 94 new and renewal awards to 57 institutions, with a median of $729K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 89% were R1s, 9% R2s and 2% RCUs.
