Museums for America

Institute of Museum and Library Services (IMLS) · Assistance Listing 45.301

IMLS obligated $14M under this program to 76 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 60 new grants and cooperative agreements to 48 institutions, with a median of $137K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 53% were R1s, 11% R2s and 11% RCUs.
