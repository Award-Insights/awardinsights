Low Income Taxpayer Clinics

Treasury (Treasury) · Assistance Listing 21.008

Treasury obligated $30M under this program to 49 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 81 new grants and cooperative agreements to 45 institutions, with a median of $42K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 60% were R1s, 16% R2s and 9% RCUs.
