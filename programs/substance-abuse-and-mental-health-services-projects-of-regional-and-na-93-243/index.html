Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Projects of Regional and National Significance

Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration (SAMHSA) · Assistance Listing 93.243

SAMHSA obligated $728M under this program to 296 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 277 new grants and cooperative agreements to 197 institutions, with a median of $250K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 43% were R1s, 16% R2s and 9% RCUs.
