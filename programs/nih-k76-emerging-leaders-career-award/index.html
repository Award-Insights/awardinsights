NIH K76: Emerging leaders career award

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code K76, all institutes

NIH awarded $48M in total cost under activity code K76 to 32 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 22 new and renewal awards to 15 institutions, with a median of $243K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 87% were R1s and 13% R2s.
