Computer and Information Science and Engineering

National Science Foundation (NSF) · Assistance Listing 47.070

NSF obligated $4.98B under this program to 451 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 4,471 new grants and cooperative agreements to 373 institutions, with a median of $300K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 48% were R1s, 25% R2s and 17% RCUs.
