Science to Achieve Results (STAR) Research Program

Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) · Assistance Listing 66.509

EPA obligated $87M under this program to 58 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 50 new grants and cooperative agreements to 40 institutions, with a median of $770K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 87% were R1s, 8% R2s and 3% RCUs.
