Outreach and Assistance for Socially Disadvantaged and Veteran Farmers and Ranchers

Agriculture (USDA) · Assistance Listing 10.443

USDA obligated $31M under this program to 36 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 21 new grants and cooperative agreements to 20 institutions, with a median of $750K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 60% were R1s, 20% R2s and 20% RCUs.
