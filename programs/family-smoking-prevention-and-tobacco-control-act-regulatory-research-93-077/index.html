National Institutes of Health (NIH) · Assistance Listing 93.077

NIH obligated $273M under this program to 61 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 26 new grants and cooperative agreements to 18 institutions, with a median of $613K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 78% were R1s and 22% R2s.
