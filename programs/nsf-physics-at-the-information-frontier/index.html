National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 755300 · Computer & Info Sci

NSF made $31M in awards (full value) under this program to 42 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 40 awards to 33 institutions, with a median of $397K (FY2026 still being revised).
