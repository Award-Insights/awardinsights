Arctic Research Support and Logistics

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 520500 · Geosciences

NSF made $68M in awards (full value) under this program to 13 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 7 awards to 7 institutions, with a median of $470K (FY2026 still being revised).
