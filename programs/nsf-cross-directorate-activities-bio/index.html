Cross-Directorate Activities (BIO)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 727500 · Bio Sciences

NSF made $494M in awards (full value) under this program to 223 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 389 awards to 159 institutions, with a median of $820K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 79% were R1s, 11% R2s and 6% RCUs.
