Plant Genome Research Program (PGRP)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 132900 · Bio Sciences

NSF made $97M in awards (full value) under this program to 70 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 80 awards to 49 institutions, with a median of $738K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 86% were R1s, 6% R2s and 4% RCUs.
