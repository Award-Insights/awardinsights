Secondary and Two-Year Postsecondary Agriculture Education Challenge Grants

Agriculture (USDA) · Assistance Listing 10.226

USDA obligated $3.0M under this program to 27 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 19 new grants and cooperative agreements to 17 institutions, with a median of $125K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 76% were R1s and 12% R2s.
