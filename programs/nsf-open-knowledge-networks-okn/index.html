Open Knowledge Networks (OKN)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 223Y00 · Tech, Innov & Partnerships

NSF made $28M in awards (full value) under this program to 15 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 18 awards to 14 institutions, with a median of $1.5M. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 93% were R1s and 7% R2s.
