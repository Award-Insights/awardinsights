Foundational Research in Robotics (FRR)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 144Y00 · Engineering

NSF made $122M in awards (full value) under this program to 108 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 164 awards to 87 institutions, with a median of $507K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 89% were R1s, 7% R2s and 2% RCUs.
