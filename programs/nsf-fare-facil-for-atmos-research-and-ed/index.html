FARE-Facil for Atmos Research & Ed

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 152900 · Geosciences

NSF made $12M in awards (full value) under this program to 26 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 25 awards to 23 institutions, with a median of $133K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 65% were R1s, 17% R2s and 4% RCUs.
