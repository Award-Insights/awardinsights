Marine Geology and Geophysics

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 162000 · Geosciences

NSF made $121M in awards (full value) under this program to 120 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 152 awards to 73 institutions, with a median of $371K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 81% were R1s, 10% R2s and 7% RCUs.
