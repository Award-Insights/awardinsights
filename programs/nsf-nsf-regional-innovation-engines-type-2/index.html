NSF Regional Innovation Engines (Type-2)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 301Y00 · Tech, Innov & Partnerships

NSF made $185M in awards (full value) under this program to 45 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 47 awards to 45 institutions, with a median of $400K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 53% were R1s, 9% R2s and 22% RCUs.
