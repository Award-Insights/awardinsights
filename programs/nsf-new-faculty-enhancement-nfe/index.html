New Faculty Enhancement (NFE)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 202Y00 · Bio Sciences

NSF made $56M in awards (full value) under this program to 105 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 67 awards to 57 institutions, with a median of $488K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 16% were R1s, 32% R2s and 26% RCUs.
