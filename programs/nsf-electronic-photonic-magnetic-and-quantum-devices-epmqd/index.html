Electronic, Photonic, Magnetic, and Quantum Devices (EPMQD)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 151700 · Engineering

NSF made $150M in awards (full value) under this program to 130 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 236 awards to 105 institutions, with a median of $408K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 90% were R1s, 8% R2s and 2% RCUs.
