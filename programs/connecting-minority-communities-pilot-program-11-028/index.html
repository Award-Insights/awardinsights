Connecting Minority Communities Pilot Program

Commerce (DOC) · Assistance Listing 11.028

DOC obligated $257M under this program to 92 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2021–23, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 93 new grants and cooperative agreements to 92 institutions, with a median of $3.0M. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 5% were R1s, 12% R2s and 18% RCUs.
