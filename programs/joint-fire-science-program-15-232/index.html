Interior (DOI) · Assistance Listing 15.232

DOI obligated $32M under this program to 54 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 79 new grants and cooperative agreements to 42 institutions, with a median of $250K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 84% were R1s, 8% R2s and 5% RCUs.
