EAR-Earth Sciences Research

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 689800 · Geosciences

NSF made $21M in awards (full value) under this program to 27 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 20 awards to 18 institutions, with a median of $774K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 78% were R1s and 22% RCUs.
