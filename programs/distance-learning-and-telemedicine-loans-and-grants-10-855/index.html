Distance Learning and Telemedicine Loans and Grants

Agriculture (USDA) · Assistance Listing 10.855

USDA obligated $38M under this program to 57 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 36 new grants and cooperative agreements to 32 institutions, with a median of $539K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 19% were R1s, 12% R2s and 9% RCUs.
