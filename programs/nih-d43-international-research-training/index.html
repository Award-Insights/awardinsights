NIH D43: International research training

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code D43, all institutes

NIH awarded $214M in total cost under activity code D43 to 68 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 48 new and renewal awards to 34 institutions, with a median of $272K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 100% were R1s.
