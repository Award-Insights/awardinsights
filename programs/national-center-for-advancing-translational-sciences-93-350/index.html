National Center for Advancing Translational Sciences

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · Assistance Listing 93.350

NIH obligated $2.56B under this program to 99 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 234 new grants and cooperative agreements to 75 institutions, with a median of $437K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 88% were R1s, 10% R2s and 1% RCUs.
