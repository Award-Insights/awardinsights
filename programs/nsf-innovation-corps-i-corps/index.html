Innovation Corps (I-Corps)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 802300 · Tech, Innov & Partnerships

NSF made $55M in awards (full value) under this program to 236 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 662 awards to 193 institutions, with a median of $50K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 73% were R1s, 15% R2s and 8% RCUs.
