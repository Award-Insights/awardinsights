Engineering Education (EngEd)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 134000 · Engineering

NSF made $64M in awards (full value) under this program to 116 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 166 awards to 81 institutions, with a median of $200K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 72% were R1s, 9% R2s and 11% RCUs.
