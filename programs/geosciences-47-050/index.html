Geosciences

National Science Foundation (NSF) · Assistance Listing 47.050

NSF obligated $3.75B under this program to 435 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 2,600 new grants and cooperative agreements to 344 institutions, with a median of $300K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 49% were R1s, 17% R2s and 17% RCUs.
