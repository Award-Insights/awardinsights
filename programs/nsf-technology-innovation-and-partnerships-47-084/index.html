NSF Technology, Innovation, and Partnerships

National Science Foundation (NSF) · Assistance Listing 47.084

NSF obligated $1.56B under this program to 433 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 1,466 new grants and cooperative agreements to 352 institutions, with a median of $50K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 48% were R1s, 16% R2s and 16% RCUs.
