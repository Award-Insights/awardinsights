Instrumentation & Facilities

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 158000 · Geosciences

NSF made $62M in awards (full value) under this program to 87 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 52 awards to 42 institutions, with a median of $392K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 71% were R1s, 7% R2s and 12% RCUs.
