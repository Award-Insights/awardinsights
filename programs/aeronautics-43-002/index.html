Aeronautics

NASA (NASA) · Assistance Listing 43.002

NASA obligated $247M under this program to 69 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 48 new grants and cooperative agreements to 41 institutions, with a median of $69K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 78% were R1s, 15% R2s and 2% RCUs.
