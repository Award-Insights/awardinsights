EDU Core Research (ECR)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 798000 · STEM Education

NSF made $263M in awards (full value) under this program to 159 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 185 awards to 105 institutions, with a median of $685K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 77% were R1s, 11% R2s and 9% RCUs.
