CSGrad4US Graduate Fellowship

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 174Y00 · Computer & Info Sci

NSF made $3.0M in awards (full value) under this program to 17 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2020–22, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 19 awards to 17 institutions, with a median of $138K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 94% were R1s and 6% RCUs.
