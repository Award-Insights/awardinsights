21st Century Cures Act - BRAIN Research Through Advancing Innovative Neurotechnologies

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · Assistance Listing 93.372

NIH obligated $531M under this program to 73 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 64 new grants and cooperative agreements to 39 institutions, with a median of $368K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 89% were R1s and 8% R2s.
