NIH R03: Small research grant

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code R03, all institutes

NIH awarded $404M in total cost under activity code R03 to 279 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 1,038 new and renewal awards to 212 institutions, with a median of $156K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 75% were R1s, 16% R2s and 9% RCUs.
