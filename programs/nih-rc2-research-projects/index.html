NIH RC2: Research projects

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code RC2, all institutes

NIH awarded $140M in total cost under activity code RC2 to 32 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 20 new and renewal awards to 16 institutions, with a median of $1.4M (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 94% were R1s and 6% R2s.
