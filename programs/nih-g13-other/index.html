NIH G13: Other

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code G13, all institutes

NIH awarded $2.4M in total cost under activity code G13 to 21 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 11 new and renewal awards to 11 institutions, with a median of $50K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 91% were R1s and 9% RCUs.
