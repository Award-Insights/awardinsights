TRIO Educational Opportunity Centers

Education (ED) · Assistance Listing 84.066

ED obligated $235M under this program to 143 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 99 new grants and cooperative agreements to 92 institutions, with a median of $300K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 16% were R1s, 10% R2s and 11% RCUs.
