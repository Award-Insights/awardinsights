Promotion of the Humanities Office of Digital Humanities

National Endowment for the Humanities (NEH) · Assistance Listing 45.169

NEH obligated $26M under this program to 113 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 170 new grants and cooperative agreements to 91 institutions, with a median of $75K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 74% were R1s, 7% R2s and 15% RCUs.
