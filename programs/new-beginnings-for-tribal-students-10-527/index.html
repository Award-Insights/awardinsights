Agriculture (USDA) · Assistance Listing 10.527

USDA obligated $22M under this program to 34 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 32 new grants and cooperative agreements to 24 institutions, with a median of $250K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 67% were R1s and 8% R2s.
