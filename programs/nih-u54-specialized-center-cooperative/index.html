NIH U54: Specialized center, cooperative

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code U54, all institutes

NIH awarded $2.93B in total cost under activity code U54 to 128 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 80 new and renewal awards to 56 institutions, with a median of $1.9M (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 75% were R1s, 16% R2s and 9% RCUs.
