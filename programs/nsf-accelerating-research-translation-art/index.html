Accelerating Research Translation (ART)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 252Y00 · Tech, Innov & Partnerships

NSF made $87M in awards (full value) under this program to 31 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 20 awards to 19 institutions, with a median of $6.0M (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 63% were R1s, 26% R2s and 11% RCUs.
