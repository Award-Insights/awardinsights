Education (ED) · Assistance Listing 84.031L
One competition of Higher Education Institutional Aid (84.031), told apart by the letter in Education's award numbers.

ED obligated $44M under this program to 42 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2021–23, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 30 new grants and cooperative agreements to 30 institutions, with a median of $377K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 30% were R1s, 20% R2s and 7% RCUs.
