Partnerships for Innovation (PFI)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 166200 · Tech, Innov & Partnerships

NSF made $90M in awards (full value) under this program to 120 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 60 awards to 51 institutions, with a median of $550K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 84% were R1s, 8% R2s and 6% RCUs.
