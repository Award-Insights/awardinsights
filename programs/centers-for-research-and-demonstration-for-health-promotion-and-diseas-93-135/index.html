Centers for Research and Demonstration for Health Promotion and Disease Prevention

Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) · Assistance Listing 93.135

CDC obligated $84M under this program to 29 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2022–24, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 16 new grants and cooperative agreements to 14 institutions, with a median of $300K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 100% were R1s.
