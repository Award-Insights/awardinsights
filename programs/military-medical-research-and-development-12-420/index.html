Military Medical Research and Development

Defense (DOD) · Assistance Listing 12.420

DOD obligated $3.91B under this program to 261 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 1,694 new grants and cooperative agreements to 203 institutions, with a median of $848K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 74% were R1s, 20% R2s and 5% RCUs.
