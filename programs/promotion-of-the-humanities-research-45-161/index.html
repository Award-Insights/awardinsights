Promotion of the Humanities Research

National Endowment for the Humanities (NEH) · Assistance Listing 45.161

NEH obligated $62M under this program to 120 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 109 new grants and cooperative agreements to 80 institutions, with a median of $196K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 61% were R1s, 13% R2s and 15% RCUs.
