National Leadership Grants

Institute of Museum and Library Services (IMLS) · Assistance Listing 45.312

IMLS obligated $51M under this program to 103 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 98 new grants and cooperative agreements to 66 institutions, with a median of $247K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 72% were R1s, 11% R2s and 6% RCUs.
