Rural Cooperative Development Grants

Agriculture (USDA) · Assistance Listing 10.771

USDA obligated $9.6M under this program to 10 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 16 new grants and cooperative agreements to 8 institutions, with a median of $200K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 50% were R1s, 12% R2s and 38% RCUs.
