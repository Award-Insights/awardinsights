Louis Stokes Alliances for Minority Participation (LSAMP)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 913300 · STEM Education

NSF made $141M in awards (full value) under this program to 77 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2022–24, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 94 awards to 77 institutions, with a median of $1.6M. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 57% were R1s, 18% R2s and 6% RCUs.
