Energy (DOE) · Assistance Listing 81.123

DOE obligated $103M under this program to 29 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 9 new grants and cooperative agreements to 8 institutions, with a median of $1.6M (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 25% were R1s and 50% R2s.
