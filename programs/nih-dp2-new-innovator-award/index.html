NIH DP2: New innovator award

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code DP2, all institutes

NIH awarded $631M in total cost under activity code DP2 to 110 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 164 new and renewal awards to 71 institutions, with a median of $801K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 92% were R1s, 6% R2s and 2% RCUs.
