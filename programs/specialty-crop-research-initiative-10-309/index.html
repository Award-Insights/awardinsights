Specialty Crop Research Initiative

Agriculture (USDA) · Assistance Listing 10.309

USDA obligated $336M under this program to 37 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 42 new grants and cooperative agreements to 20 institutions, with a median of $2.4M (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 90% were R1s, 5% R2s and 5% RCUs.
