Cancer Cause and Prevention Research

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · Assistance Listing 93.393

NIH obligated $2.76B under this program to 176 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 568 new grants and cooperative agreements to 133 institutions, with a median of $543K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 82% were R1s, 14% R2s and 3% RCUs.
