Advanced Manufacturing (AM)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 088Y00 · Engineering

NSF made $121M in awards (full value) under this program to 160 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 255 awards to 123 institutions, with a median of $350K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 82% were R1s, 15% R2s and 2% RCUs.
