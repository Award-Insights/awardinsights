Molecular Biophysics

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 114400 · Bio Sciences

NSF made $104M in awards (full value) under this program to 100 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 71 awards to 56 institutions, with a median of $800K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 71% were R1s, 16% R2s and 7% RCUs.
