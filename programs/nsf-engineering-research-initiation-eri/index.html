Engineering Research Initiation (ERI)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 180Y00 · Engineering

NSF made $35M in awards (full value) under this program to 119 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 89 awards to 60 institutions, with a median of $200K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 37% were R1s, 48% R2s and 10% RCUs.
