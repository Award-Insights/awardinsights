EPSCoR RII: Ecosystems for Collaborative Organizational Research Excellence (E-CORE)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 270Y00 · Office of Director

NSF made $105M in awards (full value) under this program to 35 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 36 awards to 35 institutions, with a median of $986K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 29% were R1s, 14% R2s and 14% RCUs.
