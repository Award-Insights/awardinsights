NIH R16: Support for research excellence (SuRE)

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code R16, all institutes

NIH awarded $176M in total cost under activity code R16 to 127 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 239 new and renewal awards to 99 institutions, with a median of $149K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 12% were R1s, 38% R2s and 32% RCUs.
