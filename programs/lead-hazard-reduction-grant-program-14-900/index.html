Lead Hazard Reduction Grant Program

Housing and Urban Development (HUD) · Assistance Listing 14.900

HUD obligated $22M under this program to 22 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 21 new grants and cooperative agreements to 19 institutions, with a median of $999K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 95% were R1s and 5% R2s.
