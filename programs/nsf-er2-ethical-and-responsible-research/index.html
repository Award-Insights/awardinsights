ER2-Ethical & Responsible Research

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 129Y00 · Social/Behav/Econ

NSF made $20M in awards (full value) under this program to 71 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 57 awards to 48 institutions, with a median of $226K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 79% were R1s, 12% R2s and 4% RCUs.
