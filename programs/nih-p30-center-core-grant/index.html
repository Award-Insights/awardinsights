NIH P30: Center core grant

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code P30, all institutes

NIH awarded $3.80B in total cost under activity code P30 to 118 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 244 new and renewal awards to 96 institutions, with a median of $1.3M (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 85% were R1s, 14% R2s and 1% RCUs.
