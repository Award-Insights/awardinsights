Autism Collaboration, Accountability, Research, Education, and Support (CARES) Act of 2014

Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA) · Assistance Listing 93.877

HRSA obligated $20M under this program to 23 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 71 new grants and cooperative agreements to 59 institutions, with a median of $458K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 80% were R1s, 15% R2s and 3% RCUs.
