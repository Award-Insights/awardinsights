Ocean Technology & Interdisc Coordination

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 168000 · Geosciences

NSF made $35M in awards (full value) under this program to 32 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 33 awards to 17 institutions, with a median of $402K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 71% were R1s, 12% R2s and 18% RCUs.
