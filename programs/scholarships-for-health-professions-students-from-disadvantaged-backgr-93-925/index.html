Scholarships for Health Professions Students from Disadvantaged Backgrounds

Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA) · Assistance Listing 93.925

HRSA obligated $191M under this program to 114 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 82 new grants and cooperative agreements to 73 institutions, with a median of $650K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 34% were R1s, 18% R2s and 11% RCUs.
