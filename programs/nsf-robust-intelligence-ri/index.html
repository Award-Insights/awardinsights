Robust Intelligence (RI)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 749500 · Computer & Info Sci

NSF made $216M in awards (full value) under this program to 146 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 282 awards to 112 institutions, with a median of $560K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 85% were R1s, 9% R2s and 4% RCUs.
