Small Business Administration (SBA) · Assistance Listing 59.043

SBA obligated $17M under this program to 19 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 44 new grants and cooperative agreements to 18 institutions, with a median of $150K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 17% were R1s, 28% R2s and 11% RCUs.
