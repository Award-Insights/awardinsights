NIH R34: Clinical trial planning

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code R34, all institutes

NIH awarded $352M in total cost under activity code R34 to 149 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 304 new and renewal awards to 107 institutions, with a median of $298K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 84% were R1s and 14% R2s.
