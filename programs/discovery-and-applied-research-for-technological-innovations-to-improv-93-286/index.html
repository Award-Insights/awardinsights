Discovery and Applied Research for Technological Innovations to Improve Human Health

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · Assistance Listing 93.286

NIH obligated $1.42B under this program to 212 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 562 new grants and cooperative agreements to 159 institutions, with a median of $363K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 78% were R1s, 15% R2s and 6% RCUs.
