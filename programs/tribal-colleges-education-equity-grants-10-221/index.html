Tribal Colleges Education Equity Grants

Agriculture (USDA) · Assistance Listing 10.221

USDA obligated $29M under this program to 34 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2022–24, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 38 new grants and cooperative agreements to 34 institutions, with a median of $157K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 6% were RCUs.
