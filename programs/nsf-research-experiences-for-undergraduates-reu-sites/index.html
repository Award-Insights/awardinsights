Research Experiences for Undergraduates (REU) Sites

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 113900 · Bio Sciences

NSF made $144M in awards (full value) under this program to 195 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 227 awards to 157 institutions, with a median of $450K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 66% were R1s, 16% R2s and 10% RCUs.
