OCE-Ocean Sciences Research

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 689900 · Geosciences

NSF made $15M in awards (full value) under this program to 24 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 19 awards to 15 institutions, with a median of $916K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 93% were R1s and 7% RCUs.
