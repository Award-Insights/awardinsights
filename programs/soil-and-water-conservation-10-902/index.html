Soil and Water Conservation

Agriculture (USDA) · Assistance Listing 10.902

USDA obligated $182M under this program to 121 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 172 new grants and cooperative agreements to 90 institutions, with a median of $400K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 54% were R1s, 17% R2s and 16% RCUs.
