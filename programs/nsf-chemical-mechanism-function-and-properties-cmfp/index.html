Chemical Mechanism, Function, and Properties (CMFP)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 910200 · Math & Physical Sci

NSF made $105M in awards (full value) under this program to 134 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 137 awards to 95 institutions, with a median of $571K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 80% were R1s, 9% R2s and 3% RCUs.
