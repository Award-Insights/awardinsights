NIH F31: Predoctoral fellowship

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code F31, all institutes

NIH awarded $433M in total cost under activity code F31 to 206 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 2,820 new and renewal awards to 180 institutions, with a median of $45K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 84% were R1s, 12% R2s and 3% RCUs.
