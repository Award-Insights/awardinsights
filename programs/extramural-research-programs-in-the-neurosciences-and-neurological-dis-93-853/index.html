Extramural Research Programs in the Neurosciences and Neurological Disorders

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · Assistance Listing 93.853

NIH obligated $7.81B under this program to 310 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 2,549 new grants and cooperative agreements to 226 institutions, with a median of $406K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 67% were R1s, 19% R2s and 10% RCUs.
