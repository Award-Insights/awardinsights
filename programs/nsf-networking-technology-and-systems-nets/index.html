Networking Technology and Systems (NeTS)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 736300 · Computer & Info Sci

NSF made $157M in awards (full value) under this program to 133 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 205 awards to 93 institutions, with a median of $360K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 90% were R1s, 9% R2s and 1% RCUs.
