Outreach Programs to Reduce the Prevalence of Obesity in High Risk Rural Areas

Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) · Assistance Listing 93.319

CDC obligated $56M under this program to 20 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2021–23, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 16 new grants and cooperative agreements to 16 institutions, with a median of $768K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 94% were R1s and 6% R2s.
