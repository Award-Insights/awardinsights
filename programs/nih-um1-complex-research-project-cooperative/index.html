NIH UM1: Complex research project, cooperative

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code UM1, all institutes

NIH awarded $3.68B in total cost under activity code UM1 to 79 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 68 new and renewal awards to 53 institutions, with a median of $3.7M (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 84% were R1s, 12% R2s and 4% RCUs.
