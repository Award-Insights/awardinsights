Physical Oceanography

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 161000 · Geosciences

NSF made $116M in awards (full value) under this program to 65 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 86 awards to 50 institutions, with a median of $542K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 90% were R1s, 4% R2s and 6% RCUs.
