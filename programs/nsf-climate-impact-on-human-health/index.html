Climate Impact on Human Health

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 300Y00 · Geosciences

NSF made $18M in awards (full value) under this program to 29 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 26 awards to 24 institutions, with a median of $300K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 75% were R1s, 12% R2s and 12% RCUs.
