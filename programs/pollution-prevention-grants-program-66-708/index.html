Pollution Prevention Grants Program

Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) · Assistance Listing 66.708

EPA obligated $38M under this program to 43 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 52 new grants and cooperative agreements to 40 institutions, with a median of $350K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 78% were R1s, 10% R2s and 8% RCUs.
