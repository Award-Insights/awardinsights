Education AND Human Resources

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 157500 · Geosciences

NSF made $22M in awards (full value) under this program to 71 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 46 awards to 42 institutions, with a median of $469K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 69% were R1s, 7% R2s and 17% RCUs.
