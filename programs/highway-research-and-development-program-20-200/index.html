Transportation (DOT) · Assistance Listing 20.200

DOT obligated $85M under this program to 23 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 11 new grants and cooperative agreements to 11 institutions, with a median of $250K (FY2026 still being revised).
