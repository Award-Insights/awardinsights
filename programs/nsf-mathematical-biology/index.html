Mathematical Biology

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 733400 · Math & Physical Sci

NSF made $57M in awards (full value) under this program to 123 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 130 awards to 82 institutions, with a median of $250K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 91% were R1s, 1% R2s and 6% RCUs.
