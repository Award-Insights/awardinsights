Arthritis, Musculoskeletal and Skin Diseases Research

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · Assistance Listing 93.846

NIH obligated $2.20B under this program to 178 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 722 new grants and cooperative agreements to 139 institutions, with a median of $367K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 81% were R1s, 15% R2s and 4% RCUs.
