Higher Education – Graduate Fellowships Grant Program

Agriculture (USDA) · Assistance Listing 10.210

USDA obligated $10M under this program to 29 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 31 new grants and cooperative agreements to 23 institutions, with a median of $238K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 96% were R1s and 4% R2s.
