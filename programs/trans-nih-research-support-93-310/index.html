Trans-NIH Research Support

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · Assistance Listing 93.310

NIH obligated $5.90B under this program to 270 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 811 new grants and cooperative agreements to 163 institutions, with a median of $400K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 80% were R1s, 14% R2s and 6% RCUs.
