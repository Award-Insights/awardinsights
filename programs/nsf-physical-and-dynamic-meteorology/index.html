Physical and Dynamic Meteorology

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 152500 · Geosciences

NSF made $106M in awards (full value) under this program to 85 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 125 awards to 60 institutions, with a median of $499K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 82% were R1s, 13% R2s and 3% RCUs.
