National STEM Teacher Corps

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 311Y00 · STEM Education

NSF made $40M in awards (full value) under this program to 17 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 17 awards to 17 institutions, with a median of $1.0M. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 65% were R1s and 35% RCUs.
