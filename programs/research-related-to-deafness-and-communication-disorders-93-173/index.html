Research Related to Deafness and Communication Disorders

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · Assistance Listing 93.173

NIH obligated $1.79B under this program to 198 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 748 new grants and cooperative agreements to 137 institutions, with a median of $268K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 80% were R1s, 16% R2s and 3% RCUs.
