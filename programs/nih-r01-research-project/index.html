NIH R01: Research project

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code R01, all institutes

NIH awarded $66.34B in total cost under activity code R01 to 407 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 12,881 new and renewal awards to 302 institutions, with a median of $653K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 65% were R1s, 23% R2s and 9% RCUs.
