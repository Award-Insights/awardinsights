NIH C06: Research facilities construction

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code C06, all institutes

NIH awarded $351M in total cost under activity code C06 to 46 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 29 new and renewal awards to 28 institutions, with a median of $7.8M (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 57% were R1s, 25% R2s and 11% RCUs.
