NIH R56: High-priority bridge

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code R56, all institutes

NIH awarded $688M in total cost under activity code R56 to 171 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 599 new and renewal awards to 141 institutions, with a median of $546K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 85% were R1s, 11% R2s and 4% RCUs.
