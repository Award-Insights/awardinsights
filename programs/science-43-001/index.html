Science

NASA (NASA) · Assistance Listing 43.001

NASA obligated $3.98B under this program to 374 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 3,778 new grants and cooperative agreements to 312 institutions, with a median of $111K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 55% were R1s, 19% R2s and 13% RCUs.
