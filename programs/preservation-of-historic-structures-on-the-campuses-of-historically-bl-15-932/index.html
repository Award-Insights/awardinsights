Preservation of Historic Structures on the Campuses of Historically Black Colleges and Universities (HBCUS).

Interior (DOI) · Assistance Listing 15.932

DOI obligated $51M under this program to 37 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 46 new grants and cooperative agreements to 21 institutions, with a median of $750K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 19% were R2s and 24% RCUs.
