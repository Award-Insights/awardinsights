Expanding Capacity in Quantum Information Science and Engineering (ExpandQISE)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 289Y00 · Math & Physical Sci

NSF made $30M in awards (full value) under this program to 26 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2022–24, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 30 awards to 26 institutions, with a median of $800K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 35% were R1s, 42% R2s and 23% RCUs.
