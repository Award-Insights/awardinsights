Arctic System Science (ARCSS)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 521900 · Geosciences

NSF made $27M in awards (full value) under this program to 47 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 35 awards to 25 institutions, with a median of $265K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 72% were R1s, 8% R2s and 4% RCUs.
