NIH P20: Exploratory center

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code P20, all institutes

NIH awarded $1.53B in total cost under activity code P20 to 89 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 89 new and renewal awards to 52 institutions, with a median of $2.3M (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 78% were R1s, 18% R2s and 4% RCUs.
