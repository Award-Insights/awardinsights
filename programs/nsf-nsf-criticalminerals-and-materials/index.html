NSF CriticalMinerals&Materials

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 357Y00 · Geosciences

NSF made $27M in awards (full value) under this program to 44 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 58 awards to 44 institutions, with a median of $353K (FY2026 still being revised).
