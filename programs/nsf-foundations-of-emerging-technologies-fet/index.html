Foundations of Emerging Technologies (FET)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 089Y00 · Computer & Info Sci

NSF made $83M in awards (full value) under this program to 94 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 126 awards to 67 institutions, with a median of $499K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 94% were R1s, 4% R2s and 1% RCUs.
