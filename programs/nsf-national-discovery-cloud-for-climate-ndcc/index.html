National Discovery Cloud for Climate (NDCC)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 295Y00 · Computer & Info Sci

NSF made $26M in awards (full value) under this program to 49 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 59 awards to 47 institutions, with a median of $596K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 94% were R1s, 2% R2s and 2% RCUs.
