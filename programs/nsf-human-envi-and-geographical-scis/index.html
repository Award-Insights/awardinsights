Human-Envi & Geographical Scis

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 141Y00 · Social/Behav/Econ

NSF made $27M in awards (full value) under this program to 70 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 50 awards to 42 institutions, with a median of $400K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 81% were R1s, 10% R2s and 7% RCUs.
