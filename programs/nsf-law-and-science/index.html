Law & Science

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 128Y00 · Social/Behav/Econ

NSF made $23M in awards (full value) under this program to 62 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 42 awards to 34 institutions, with a median of $243K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 82% were R1s, 6% R2s and 12% RCUs.
