NIH P50: Specialized center

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code P50, all institutes

NIH awarded $1.53B in total cost under activity code P50 to 77 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 67 new and renewal awards to 47 institutions, with a median of $2.1M (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 93% were R1s, 2% R2s and 4% RCUs.
