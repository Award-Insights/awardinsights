Interfacial Engineering

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 141700 · Engineering

NSF made $42M in awards (full value) under this program to 73 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 65 awards to 48 institutions, with a median of $431K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 90% were R1s, 4% R2s and 2% RCUs.
