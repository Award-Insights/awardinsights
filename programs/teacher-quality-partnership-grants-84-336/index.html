Teacher Quality Partnership Grants

Education (ED) · Assistance Listing 84.336

ED obligated $148M under this program to 70 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 22 new grants and cooperative agreements to 20 institutions, with a median of $1.4M (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 30% were R1s, 15% R2s and 35% RCUs.
