NIH K99: Transition to independence (K99/R00)

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code K99, all institutes

NIH awarded $377M in total cost under activity code K99 to 149 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 999 new and renewal awards to 117 institutions, with a median of $125K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 86% were R1s, 10% R2s and 4% RCUs.
