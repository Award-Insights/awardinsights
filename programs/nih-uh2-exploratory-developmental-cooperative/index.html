NIH UH2: Exploratory/developmental, cooperative

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code UH2, all institutes

NIH awarded $19M in total cost under activity code UH2 to 31 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 15 new and renewal awards to 13 institutions, with a median of $233K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 100% were R1s.
