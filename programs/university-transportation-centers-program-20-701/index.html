Transportation (DOT) · Assistance Listing 20.701

DOT obligated $334M under this program to 35 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2022–24, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 40 new grants and cooperative agreements to 35 institutions, with a median of $2.0M. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 71% were R1s and 29% R2s.
