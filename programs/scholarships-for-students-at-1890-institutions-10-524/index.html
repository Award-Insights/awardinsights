Scholarships for Students at 1890 Institutions

Agriculture (USDA) · Assistance Listing 10.524

USDA obligated $69M under this program to 19 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 95 new grants and cooperative agreements to 19 institutions, with a median of $505K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 42% were R2s and 58% RCUs.
