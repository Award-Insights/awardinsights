CET Strategic Investments

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 268Y00 · Engineering

NSF made $24M in awards (full value) under this program to 42 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 53 awards to 42 institutions, with a median of $300K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 88% were R1s, 7% R2s and 5% RCUs.
