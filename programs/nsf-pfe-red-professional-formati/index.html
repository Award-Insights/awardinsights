PFE\RED - Professional Formati

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 012Y00 · Engineering

NSF made $9.9M in awards (full value) under this program to 25 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 21 awards to 21 institutions, with a median of $150K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 48% were R1s, 29% R2s and 10% RCUs.
