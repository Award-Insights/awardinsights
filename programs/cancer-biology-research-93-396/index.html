Cancer Biology Research

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · Assistance Listing 93.396

NIH obligated $3.41B under this program to 206 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 680 new grants and cooperative agreements to 151 institutions, with a median of $521K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 77% were R1s, 13% R2s and 7% RCUs.
