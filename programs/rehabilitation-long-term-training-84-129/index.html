Rehabilitation Long-Term Training

Education (ED) · Assistance Listing 84.129

ED obligated $71M under this program to 68 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 76 new grants and cooperative agreements to 49 institutions, with a median of $200K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 47% were R1s, 27% R2s and 12% RCUs.
