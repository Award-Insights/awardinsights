Oral Diseases and Disorders Research

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · Assistance Listing 93.121

NIH obligated $1.73B under this program to 190 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 754 new grants and cooperative agreements to 140 institutions, with a median of $318K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 79% were R1s, 15% R2s and 5% RCUs.
