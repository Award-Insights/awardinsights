Engineering for Civil Infrastructure (ECI)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 073Y00 · Engineering

NSF made $80M in awards (full value) under this program to 112 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 142 awards to 85 institutions, with a median of $393K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 87% were R1s, 8% R2s and 4% RCUs.
