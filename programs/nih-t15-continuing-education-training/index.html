NIH T15: Continuing education training

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code T15, all institutes

NIH awarded $53M in total cost under activity code T15 to 18 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2020–22, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 18 new and renewal awards to 18 institutions, with a median of $611K.
