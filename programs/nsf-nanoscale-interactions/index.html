Nanoscale Interactions

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 117900 · Engineering

NSF made $33M in awards (full value) under this program to 70 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 52 awards to 43 institutions, with a median of $405K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 84% were R1s, 9% R2s and 7% RCUs.
