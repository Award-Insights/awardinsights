NIH K26: Midcareer investigator award

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code K26, all institutes

NIH awarded $5.4M in total cost under activity code K26 to 14 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2021–23, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 12 new and renewal awards to 12 institutions, with a median of $106K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 92% were R1s and 8% R2s.
