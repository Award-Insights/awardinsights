NIH RM1: Research project with complex structure

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code RM1, all institutes

NIH awarded $474M in total cost under activity code RM1 to 53 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 35 new and renewal awards to 27 institutions, with a median of $1.8M (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 93% were R1s and 7% R2s.
