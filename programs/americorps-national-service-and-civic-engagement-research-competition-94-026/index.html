Americorps National Service and Civic Engagement Research Competition 94.026

AmeriCorps (Corporation for National and Community Service) (AmeriCorps) · Assistance Listing 94.026

AmeriCorps obligated $46M under this program to 26 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 23 new grants and cooperative agreements to 21 institutions, with a median of $1.0M (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 71% were R1s, 14% R2s and 5% RCUs.
