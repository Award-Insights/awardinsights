Undergraduate Programs IN Chemistry

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 198600 · Math & Physical Sci

NSF made $17M in awards (full value) under this program to 48 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 34 awards to 34 institutions, with a median of $460K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 82% were R1s, 12% R2s and 3% RCUs.
