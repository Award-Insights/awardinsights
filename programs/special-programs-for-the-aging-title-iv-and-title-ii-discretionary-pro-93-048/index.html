Special Programs for the Aging, Title IV, and Title II, Discretionary Projects

HHS: other divisions (HHS other) · Assistance Listing 93.048

HHS other obligated $27M under this program to 24 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 15 new grants and cooperative agreements to 13 institutions, with a median of $385K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 77% were R1s, 15% R2s and 8% RCUs.
