NSF Regional Innovation Engines (Type-1)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 200Y00 · Tech, Innov & Partnerships

NSF made $104M in awards (full value) under this program to 95 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2022–24, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 97 awards to 95 institutions, with a median of $800K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 43% were R1s, 9% R2s and 9% RCUs.
