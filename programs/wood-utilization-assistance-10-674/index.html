Wood Utilization Assistance

Agriculture (USDA) · Assistance Listing 10.674

USDA obligated $17M under this program to 47 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 31 new grants and cooperative agreements to 29 institutions, with a median of $299K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 66% were R1s, 14% R2s and 7% RCUs.
