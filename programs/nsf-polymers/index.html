Polymers

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 177300 · Math & Physical Sci

NSF made $57M in awards (full value) under this program to 69 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 79 awards to 55 institutions, with a median of $500K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 89% were R1s, 4% R2s and 5% RCUs.
