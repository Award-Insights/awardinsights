Cross-Directorate Activities

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 139700 · Social/Behav/Econ

NSF made $74M in awards (full value) under this program to 89 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 117 awards to 79 institutions, with a median of $600K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 86% were R1s, 8% R2s and 5% RCUs.
