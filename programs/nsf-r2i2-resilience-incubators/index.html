R2I2: Resilience Incubators

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 298Y00 · Geosciences

NSF made $6.7M in awards (full value) under this program to 23 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 24 awards to 23 institutions, with a median of $263K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 78% were R1s, 13% R2s and 4% RCUs.
