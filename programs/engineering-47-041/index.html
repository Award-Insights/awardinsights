Engineering

National Science Foundation (NSF) · Assistance Listing 47.041

NSF obligated $3.55B under this program to 398 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 4,306 new grants and cooperative agreements to 340 institutions, with a median of $332K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 51% were R1s, 26% R2s and 12% RCUs.
