Postdoctoral Fellowships

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 713700 · Geosciences

NSF made $39M in awards (full value) under this program to 67 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 45 awards to 41 institutions, with a median of $353K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 76% were R1s, 17% R2s and 5% RCUs.
