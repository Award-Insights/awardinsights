NIH R24: Resource-related research

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code R24, all institutes

NIH awarded $684M in total cost under activity code R24 to 119 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 124 new and renewal awards to 72 institutions, with a median of $696K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 83% were R1s and 17% R2s.
