Mathematical and Physical Sciences

National Science Foundation (NSF) · Assistance Listing 47.049

NSF obligated $6.44B under this program to 548 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 6,207 new grants and cooperative agreements to 455 institutions, with a median of $253K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 40% were R1s, 22% R2s and 19% RCUs.
