Measurement and Engineering Research and Standards

Commerce (DOC) · Assistance Listing 11.609

DOC obligated $250M under this program to 116 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 120 new grants and cooperative agreements to 67 institutions, with a median of $132K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 88% were R1s, 9% R2s and 2% RCUs.
