Tribally Controlled Colleges and Universities (Title III, Part A)

Education (ED) · Assistance Listing 84.031T
One competition of Higher Education Institutional Aid (84.031), told apart by the letter in Education's award numbers.

ED obligated $305M under this program to 35 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 34 new grants and cooperative agreements to 34 institutions, with a median of $1.2M. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 6% were RCUs.
