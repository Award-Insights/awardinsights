Cancer Research Manpower

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · Assistance Listing 93.398

NIH obligated $846M under this program to 143 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 1,152 new grants and cooperative agreements to 107 institutions, with a median of $53K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 83% were R1s, 14% R2s and 2% RCUs.
