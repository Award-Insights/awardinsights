Rural Health and Safety Education Competitive Grants Program

Agriculture (USDA) · Assistance Listing 10.516

USDA obligated $20M under this program to 32 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 26 new grants and cooperative agreements to 20 institutions, with a median of $350K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 85% were R1s and 15% R2s.
