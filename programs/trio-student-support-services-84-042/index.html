TRIO Student Support Services

Education (ED) · Assistance Listing 84.042

ED obligated $1.86B under this program to 959 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 1,180 new grants and cooperative agreements to 885 institutions, with a median of $286K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 11% were R1s, 7% R2s and 8% RCUs.
