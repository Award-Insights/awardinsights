Cooperative Agreements to Support State-Based Safe Motherhood and Infant Health Initiative Programs

Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) · Assistance Listing 93.946

CDC obligated $22M under this program to 19 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 71% were R1s and 29% R2s.
