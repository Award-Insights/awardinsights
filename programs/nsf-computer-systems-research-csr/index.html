Computer Systems Research (CSR)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 735400 · Computer & Info Sci

NSF made $164M in awards (full value) under this program to 140 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 200 awards to 108 institutions, with a median of $500K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 86% were R1s, 11% R2s and 3% RCUs.
