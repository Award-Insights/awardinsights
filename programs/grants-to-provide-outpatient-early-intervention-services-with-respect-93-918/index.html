Grants to Provide Outpatient Early Intervention Services with Respect to HIV Disease

Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA) · Assistance Listing 93.918

HRSA obligated $144M under this program to 52 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2021–23, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 13 new grants and cooperative agreements to 10 institutions, with a median of $150K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 40% were R1s, 40% R2s and 10% RCUs.
