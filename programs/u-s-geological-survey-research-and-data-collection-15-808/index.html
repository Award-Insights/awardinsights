Interior (DOI) · Assistance Listing 15.808

DOI obligated $547M under this program to 190 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 291 new grants and cooperative agreements to 124 institutions, with a median of $100K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 70% were R1s, 13% R2s and 11% RCUs.
