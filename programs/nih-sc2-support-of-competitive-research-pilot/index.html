NIH SC2: Support of competitive research, pilot

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code SC2, all institutes

NIH awarded $13M in total cost under activity code SC2 to 30 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2020–22, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 9 new and renewal awards to 9 institutions, with a median of $146K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 11% were R1s, 44% R2s and 33% RCUs.
