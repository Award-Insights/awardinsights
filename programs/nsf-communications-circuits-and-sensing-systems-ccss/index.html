Communications, Circuits, and Sensing-Systems (CCSS)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 756400 · Engineering

NSF made $167M in awards (full value) under this program to 147 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 295 awards to 125 institutions, with a median of $400K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 88% were R1s, 11% R2s and 1% RCUs.
