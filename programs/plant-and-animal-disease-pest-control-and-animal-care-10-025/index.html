Agriculture (USDA) · Assistance Listing 10.025

USDA obligated $514M under this program to 132 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 834 new grants and cooperative agreements to 115 institutions, with a median of $115K (FY2026 still being revised).
