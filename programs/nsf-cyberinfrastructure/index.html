Cyberinfrastructure

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 723100 · Computer & Info Sci

NSF made $53M in awards (full value) under this program to 49 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 17 awards to 17 institutions, with a median of $380K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 94% were R1s and 6% RCUs.
