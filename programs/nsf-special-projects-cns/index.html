Special Projects - CNS

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 171400 · Computer & Info Sci

NSF made $106M in awards (full value) under this program to 142 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 61 awards to 49 institutions, with a median of $457K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 96% were R1s and 4% R2s.
