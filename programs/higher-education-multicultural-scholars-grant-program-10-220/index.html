Higher Education - Multicultural Scholars Grant Program

Agriculture (USDA) · Assistance Listing 10.220

USDA obligated $4.8M under this program to 19 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2022–24, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 20 new grants and cooperative agreements to 19 institutions, with a median of $248K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 74% were R1s, 21% R2s and 5% RCUs.
