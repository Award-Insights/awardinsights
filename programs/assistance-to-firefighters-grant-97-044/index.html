Assistance to Firefighters Grant

Homeland Security (DHS) · Assistance Listing 97.044

DHS obligated $51M under this program to 31 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 44 new grants and cooperative agreements to 25 institutions, with a median of $586K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 88% were R1s and 8% R2s.
