Medical Library Assistance

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · Assistance Listing 93.879

NIH obligated $316M under this program to 105 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 109 new grants and cooperative agreements to 61 institutions, with a median of $332K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 90% were R1s, 3% R2s and 5% RCUs.
