Environmental Health

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · Assistance Listing 93.113

NIH obligated $1.88B under this program to 209 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 596 new grants and cooperative agreements to 146 institutions, with a median of $420K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 79% were R1s, 13% R2s and 6% RCUs.
