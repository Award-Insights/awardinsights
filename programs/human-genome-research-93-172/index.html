Human Genome Research

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · Assistance Listing 93.172

NIH obligated $1.68B under this program to 139 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 243 new grants and cooperative agreements to 87 institutions, with a median of $452K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 74% were R1s, 14% R2s and 9% RCUs.
