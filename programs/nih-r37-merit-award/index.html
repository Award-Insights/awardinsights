NIH R37: MERIT award

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code R37, all institutes

NIH awarded $1.35B in total cost under activity code R37 to 126 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 207 new and renewal awards to 83 institutions, with a median of $595K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 87% were R1s, 11% R2s and 1% RCUs.
