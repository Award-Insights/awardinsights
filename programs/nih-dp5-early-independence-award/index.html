NIH DP5: Early independence award

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code DP5, all institutes

NIH awarded $112M in total cost under activity code DP5 to 47 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 25 new and renewal awards to 18 institutions, with a median of $398K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 94% were R1s and 6% R2s.
