NIH S15: Instruments and other

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code S15, all institutes

NIH awarded $9.5M in total cost under activity code S15 to 35 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 36 new and renewal awards to 35 institutions, with a median of $327K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 79% were R1s, 15% R2s and 3% RCUs.
