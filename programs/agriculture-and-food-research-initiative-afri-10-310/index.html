Agriculture and Food Research Initiative (AFRI)

Agriculture (USDA) · Assistance Listing 10.310

USDA obligated $2.01B under this program to 289 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 1,609 new grants and cooperative agreements to 208 institutions, with a median of $500K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 61% were R1s, 21% R2s and 11% RCUs.
