Polar Special Initiatives

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 017Y00 · Geosciences

NSF made $10M in awards (full value) under this program to 18 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 15 awards to 13 institutions, with a median of $313K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 69% were R1s, 8% R2s and 8% RCUs.
