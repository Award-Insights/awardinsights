Conservation and Rehabilitation of Natural Resources on Military Installations

Defense (DOD) · Assistance Listing 12.005

DOD obligated $478M under this program to 39 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 84 new grants and cooperative agreements to 27 institutions, with a median of $339K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 84% were R1s, 8% R2s and 4% RCUs.
