National AI Research Resource Pilot (NAIRR)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 296Y00 · Computer & Info Sci

NSF made $147M in awards (full value) under this program to 58 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 68 awards to 54 institutions, with a median of $300K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 78% were R1s, 9% R2s and 9% RCUs.
