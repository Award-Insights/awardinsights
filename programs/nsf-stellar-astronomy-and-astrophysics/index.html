Stellar Astronomy and Astrophysics

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 121500 · Math & Physical Sci

NSF made $45M in awards (full value) under this program to 80 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 89 awards to 60 institutions, with a median of $390K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 80% were R1s, 7% R2s and 8% RCUs.
