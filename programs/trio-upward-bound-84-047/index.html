TRIO Upward Bound

Education (ED) · Assistance Listing 84.047

ED obligated $2.05B under this program to 629 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2022–24, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 1,174 new grants and cooperative agreements to 622 institutions, with a median of $298K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 18% were R1s, 10% R2s and 11% RCUs.
