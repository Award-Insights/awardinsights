EPSCoR RII: Focused EPSCoR Collaborations (RII-FEC)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 194Y00 · Office of Director

NSF made $247M in awards (full value) under this program to 56 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 70 awards to 44 institutions, with a median of $1.9M (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 64% were R1s, 20% R2s and 14% RCUs.
