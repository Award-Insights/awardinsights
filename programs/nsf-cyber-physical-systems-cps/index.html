Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 791800 · Computer & Info Sci

NSF made $141M in awards (full value) under this program to 108 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 163 awards to 83 institutions, with a median of $499K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 86% were R1s, 10% R2s and 5% RCUs.
