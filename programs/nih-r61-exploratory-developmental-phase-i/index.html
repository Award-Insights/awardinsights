NIH R61: Exploratory/developmental, phase I

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code R61, all institutes

NIH awarded $600M in total cost under activity code R61 to 140 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 343 new and renewal awards to 114 institutions, with a median of $582K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 88% were R1s, 9% R2s and 3% RCUs.
