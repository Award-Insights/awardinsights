Advanced Research Projects Agency - Energy

Energy (DOE) · Assistance Listing 81.135

DOE obligated $722M under this program to 113 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 210 new grants and cooperative agreements to 95 institutions, with a median of $1.5M (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 91% were R1s, 5% R2s and 3% RCUs.
