National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 748700 · Math & Physical Sci

NSF made $4.0M in awards (full value) under this program to 19 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2022–24, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 21 awards to 19 institutions, with a median of $249K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 53% were R1s, 37% R2s and 11% RCUs.
