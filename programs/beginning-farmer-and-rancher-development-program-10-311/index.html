Beginning Farmer and Rancher Development Program

Agriculture (USDA) · Assistance Listing 10.311

USDA obligated $34M under this program to 45 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 28 new grants and cooperative agreements to 28 institutions, with a median of $749K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 67% were R1s, 11% R2s and 19% RCUs.
