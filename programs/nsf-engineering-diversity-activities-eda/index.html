Engineering Diversity Activities (EDA)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 768000 · Engineering

NSF made $35M in awards (full value) under this program to 53 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 48 awards to 31 institutions, with a median of $400K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 77% were R1s, 13% R2s and 6% RCUs.
