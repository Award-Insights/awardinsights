Scientific Research - Combating Weapons of Mass Destruction

Defense (DOD) · Assistance Listing 12.351

DOD obligated $194M under this program to 53 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 26 new grants and cooperative agreements to 22 institutions, with a median of $180K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 100% were R1s.
