Engineering of Biomedical Systems (EBMS)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 534500 · Engineering

NSF made $60M in awards (full value) under this program to 103 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 100 awards to 67 institutions, with a median of $500K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 81% were R1s, 16% R2s and 3% RCUs.
