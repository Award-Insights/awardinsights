Partnerships for Research and Education in Materials (PREM)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 791300 · Math & Physical Sci

NSF made $31M in awards (full value) under this program to 15 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2022–24, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 15 awards to 15 institutions, with a median of $4.2M. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 40% were R1s, 47% R2s and 13% RCUs.
