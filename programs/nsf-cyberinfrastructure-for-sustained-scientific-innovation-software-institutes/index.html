Cyberinfrastructure for Sustained Scientific Innovation: Software Institutes

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 800400 · Computer & Info Sci

NSF made $188M in awards (full value) under this program to 139 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 234 awards to 115 institutions, with a median of $580K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 83% were R1s, 13% R2s and 3% RCUs.
