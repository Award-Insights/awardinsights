NIH K08: Mentored clinical scientist career award

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code K08, all institutes

NIH awarded $696M in total cost under activity code K08 to 106 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 461 new and renewal awards to 76 institutions, with a median of $175K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 82% were R1s, 16% R2s and 3% RCUs.
