Robert Noyce Teacher Scholarship Program

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 179500 · STEM Education

NSF made $308M in awards (full value) under this program to 236 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 159 awards to 130 institutions, with a median of $985K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 43% were R1s, 15% R2s and 17% RCUs.
