Agriculture (USDA) · Assistance Listing 10.868

USDA obligated $8.9M under this program to 34 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2023–25, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 49 new grants and cooperative agreements to 33 institutions, with a median of $100K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 58% were R1s, 21% R2s and 12% RCUs.
