Education (ED) · Assistance Listing 84.031M
One competition of Higher Education Institutional Aid (84.031), told apart by the letter in Education's award numbers.

ED obligated $69M under this program to 50 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2022–24, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 37 new grants and cooperative agreements to 36 institutions, with a median of $600K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 8% were R1s, 6% R2s and 22% RCUs.
