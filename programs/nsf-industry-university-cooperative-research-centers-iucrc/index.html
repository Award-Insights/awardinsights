Industry-University Cooperative Research Centers (IUCRC)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 576100 · Engineering

NSF made $59M in awards (full value) under this program to 100 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 99 awards to 71 institutions, with a median of $250K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 83% were R1s and 17% R2s.
