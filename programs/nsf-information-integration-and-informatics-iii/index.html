Information Integration and Informatics (III)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 736400 · Computer & Info Sci

NSF made $211M in awards (full value) under this program to 165 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 311 awards to 133 institutions, with a median of $400K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 83% were R1s, 14% R2s and 3% RCUs.
