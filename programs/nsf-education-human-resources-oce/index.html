Education/Human Resources, OCE

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 169000 · Geosciences

NSF made $21M in awards (full value) under this program to 48 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 21 awards to 20 institutions, with a median of $480K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 65% were R1s, 15% R2s and 10% RCUs.
