Integrative Activities

National Science Foundation (NSF) · Assistance Listing 47.083

NSF obligated $2.09B under this program to 418 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 1,303 new grants and cooperative agreements to 339 institutions, with a median of $295K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 42% were R1s, 18% R2s and 21% RCUs.
