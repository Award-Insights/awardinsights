Injury Prevention and Control Research and State and Community Based Programs

CDC obligated $255M under this program to 70 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 75 new grants and cooperative agreements to 45 institutions, with a median of $400K (FY2026 still being revised).
