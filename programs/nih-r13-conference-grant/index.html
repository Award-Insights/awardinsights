NIH R13: Conference grant

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code R13, all institutes

NIH awarded $29M in total cost under activity code R13 to 159 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 417 new and renewal awards to 129 institutions, with a median of $22K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 78% were R1s, 13% R2s and 7% RCUs.
