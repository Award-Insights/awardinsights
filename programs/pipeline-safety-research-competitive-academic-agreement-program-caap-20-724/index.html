Pipeline Safety Research Competitive Academic Agreement Program (CAAP)

Transportation (DOT) · Assistance Listing 20.724

DOT obligated $16M under this program to 17 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 13 new grants and cooperative agreements to 9 institutions, with a median of $709K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 67% were R1s and 22% R2s.
