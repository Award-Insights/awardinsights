Basic, Applied, and Advanced Research in Science and Engineering

Defense (DOD) · Assistance Listing 12.630

DOD obligated $1000M under this program to 236 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 380 new grants and cooperative agreements to 171 institutions, with a median of $398K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 54% were R1s, 21% R2s and 15% RCUs.
