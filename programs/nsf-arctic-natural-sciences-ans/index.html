Arctic Natural Sciences (ANS)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 528000 · Geosciences

NSF made $60M in awards (full value) under this program to 81 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 88 awards to 50 institutions, with a median of $357K (FY2026 still being revised).
