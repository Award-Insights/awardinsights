Conservation Stewardship Program

Agriculture (USDA) · Assistance Listing 10.924

USDA obligated $14M under this program to 17 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 8 new grants and cooperative agreements to 7 institutions (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 57% were R1s, 14% R2s and 29% RCUs.
