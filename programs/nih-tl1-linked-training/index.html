NIH TL1: Linked training

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code TL1, all institutes

NIH awarded $129M in total cost under activity code TL1 to 53 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 10 new and renewal awards to 10 institutions, with a median of $714K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 100% were R1s.
