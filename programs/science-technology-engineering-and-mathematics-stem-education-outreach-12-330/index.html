Science, Technology, Engineering & Mathematics (STEM) Education, Outreach and Workforce Program

Defense (DOD) · Assistance Listing 12.330

DOD obligated $98M under this program to 56 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 31 new grants and cooperative agreements to 27 institutions, with a median of $100K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 74% were R1s, 15% R2s and 4% RCUs.
