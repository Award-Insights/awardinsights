NIH SC3: Support of competitive research, pilot

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code SC3, all institutes

NIH awarded $19M in total cost under activity code SC3 to 35 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2020–22, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 13 new and renewal awards to 11 institutions, with a median of $106K. Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 27% were R2s and 64% RCUs.
