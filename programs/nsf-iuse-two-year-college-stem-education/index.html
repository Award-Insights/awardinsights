IUSE: Two-Year College STEM Education

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 264Y00 · STEM Education

NSF made $38M in awards (full value) under this program to 79 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 88 awards to 72 institutions, with a median of $460K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 7% were R1s, 3% R2s and 7% RCUs.
