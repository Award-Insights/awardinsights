Chemical Oceanography

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 167000 · Geosciences

NSF made $98M in awards (full value) under this program to 82 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 134 awards to 60 institutions, with a median of $479K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 83% were R1s, 12% R2s and 5% RCUs.
