Historically Black Colleges and Universities Undergraduate Program (HBCU-UP)

National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 159400 · STEM Education

NSF made $170M in awards (full value) under this program to 84 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 151 awards to 64 institutions, with a median of $400K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 12% were R1s, 20% R2s and 33% RCUs.
