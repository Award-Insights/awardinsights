NIH R21: Exploratory/developmental research

National Institutes of Health (NIH) · activity code R21, all institutes

NIH awarded $3.84B in total cost under activity code R21 to 336 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 4,684 new and renewal awards to 271 institutions, with a median of $276K (FY2026 still being revised). Of the Carnegie institutions that received one, 69% were R1s, 19% R2s and 11% RCUs.
