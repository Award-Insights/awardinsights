National Science Foundation (NSF) · program 177500 · Math & Physical Sci

NSF made $99M in awards (full value) under this program to 100 colleges, universities and research organizations in FY2022–26. In FY2024–26, its latest fiscal year with awards and the two before it, there were 140 awards to 82 institutions, with a median of $513K (FY2026 still being revised).
