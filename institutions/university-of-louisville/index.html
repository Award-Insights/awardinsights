University of Louisville

Louisville, Kentucky · Public · Carnegie R1

University of Louisville received $520M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 65% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $100M (still being revised), against $105M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $247M in FY2025, 38% federally funded.

What’s counted

The sources behind this page, and which recipients its award figures include

Sources

  • Federal awards: USAspending.gov, prime grants, cooperative agreements and contracts, FY2022 through September 30, 2026
  • NIH awards: NIH RePORTER
  • NSF awards: NSF's award records
  • R&D spending: NSF's HERD survey, as the institution reports it
  • Research designation: Carnegie Classification, 2025

Recipients counted

Federal, NIH and NSF count awards made to these recipients, named as the records name them. Money University of Louisville receives as a subaward from another organization isn't among them.
