Western Michigan University

Kalamazoo, Michigan · Public · Carnegie R2

Western Michigan University received $110M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 55% of it from the Department of Education. In FY2026 it received $19M (still being revised), against $24M a year in FY2022–24.

What’s counted

The sources behind this page, and which recipients its award figures include

Sources

  • Federal awards: USAspending.gov, prime grants, cooperative agreements and contracts, FY2022 through September 30, 2026
  • NIH awards: NIH RePORTER
  • NSF awards: NSF's award records
  • R&D spending: NSF's HERD survey, as the institution reports it, together with other campuses as Western Michigan University (incl. Homer Stryker M.D. School of Medicine)
  • Research designation: Carnegie Classification, 2025

Recipients counted

Federal, NIH and NSF count awards made to these recipients, named as the records name them. Money Western Michigan University receives as a subaward from another organization isn't among them.
