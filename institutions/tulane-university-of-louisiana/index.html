Tulane University of Louisiana

New Orleans, Louisiana · Private nonprofit · Carnegie R1

Tulane University of Louisiana received $696M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 77% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $129M (still being revised), against $147M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $307M in FY2025, 55% federally funded.
