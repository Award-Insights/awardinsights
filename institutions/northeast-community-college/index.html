Northeast Community College

Norfolk, Nebraska · Public

Northeast Community College received $16M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 59% of it from the Department of Agriculture. In FY2026 it received $272K (still being revised), against $5.1M a year in FY2022–24.
