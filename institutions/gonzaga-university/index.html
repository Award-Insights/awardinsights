Gonzaga University

Spokane, Washington · Private nonprofit

Gonzaga University received $25M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 85% of it from the Environmental Protection Agency. In FY2026 it received $897K (still being revised), against $1.1M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $1.0M in FY2025, 81% federally funded.
