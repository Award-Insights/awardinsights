Dominican University

River Forest, Illinois · Private nonprofit

Dominican University received $22M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 61% of it from the Department of Education. In FY2026 it received $720K (still being revised), against $6.0M a year in FY2022–24.
