Georgia College & State University

Milledgeville, Georgia · Public

Georgia College & State University received $8.2M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 65% of it from the Health Resources and Services Administration. In FY2026 it received $1.6M (still being revised), against $1.9M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $7.8M in FY2025, 6% federally funded.
