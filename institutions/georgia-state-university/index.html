Georgia State University

Atlanta, Georgia · Public · Carnegie R1

Georgia State University received $443M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 39% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $71M (still being revised), against $98M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $255M in FY2025, 39% federally funded.
