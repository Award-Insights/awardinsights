Louisiana State University and Agricultural & Mechanical College

Baton Rouge, Louisiana · Public · Carnegie R1

Louisiana State University and Agricultural & Mechanical College received $563M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 31% of it from the National Science Foundation. In FY2026 it received $114M (still being revised), against $114M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $452M in FY2025, 34% federally funded.
