University of Alabama at Birmingham

Birmingham, Alabama · Public · Carnegie R1

University of Alabama at Birmingham received $1.96B in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 80% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $396M (still being revised), against $403M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $915M in FY2025, 55% federally funded.
