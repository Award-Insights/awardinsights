Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution

Woods Hole, Massachusetts

Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution received $978M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 65% of it from the National Science Foundation. In FY2026 it received $163M (still being revised), against $211M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $289M in FY2025, 81% federally funded.
