University of Southern Maine

Portland, Maine · Public · Carnegie RCU

University of Southern Maine received $61M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 43% of it from the Environmental Protection Agency. In FY2026 it received $9.4M (still being revised), against $12M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $7.0M in FY2025, 42% federally funded.

What’s counted

The sources behind this page, and which recipients its award figures include

Sources

  • Federal awards: USAspending.gov, prime grants, cooperative agreements and contracts, FY2022 through September 30, 2026
  • NIH awards: NIH RePORTER

Recipients counted

Federal, NIH and NSF count awards made to these recipients, named as the records name them. Money University of Southern Maine receives as a subaward from another organization isn't among them.
