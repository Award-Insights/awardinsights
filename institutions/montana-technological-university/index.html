Montana Technological University

Butte, Montana · Public · Carnegie RCU

Montana Technological University received $83M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 54% of it from the Department of Defense. In FY2026 it received $4.8M (still being revised), against $16M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $22M in FY2025, 58% federally funded.
