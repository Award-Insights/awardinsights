Philadelphia College of Osteopathic Medicine

Philadelphia, Pennsylvania · Private nonprofit · Carnegie RCU

Philadelphia College of Osteopathic Medicine received $7.1M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 78% of it from the Department of Defense. In FY2026 it received $1.8M (still being revised), against $1.2M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $9.1M in FY2025, 9% federally funded.
