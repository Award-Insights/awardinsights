Missouri University of Science and Technology

Rolla, Missouri · Public · Carnegie R1

Missouri University of Science and Technology received $119M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 31% of it from the National Science Foundation. In FY2026 it received $27M (still being revised), against $25M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $78M in FY2025, 54% federally funded.

What’s counted

The sources behind this page, and which recipients its award figures include

Sources

  • Federal awards: USAspending.gov, prime grants, cooperative agreements and contracts, FY2022 through September 30, 2026
  • NIH awards: NIH RePORTER
  • NSF awards: NSF's award records
  • R&D spending: NSF's HERD survey, as the institution reports it
  • Research designation: Carnegie Classification, 2025

Recipients counted

Federal, NIH and NSF count awards made to these recipients, named as the records name them. Money Missouri University of Science and Technology receives as a subaward from another organization isn't among them.

  • UNIVERSITY OF MISSOURI SYSTEMFederal · NIH · NSFAlso recorded as MISSOURI UNIVERSITY OF SCIENCE & TECHNOLOGY; MISSOURI UNIVERSITY OF SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGYRegistered under the legal name of its system or governing board; this registration is Missouri University of Science and Technology's.

Something counted wrongly? Write to info@awardinsights.org.
