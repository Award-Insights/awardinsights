Oregon State University

Corvallis, Oregon · Public · Carnegie R1

Oregon State University received $989M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 30% of it from the National Science Foundation. In FY2026 it received $146M (still being revised), against $226M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $395M in FY2025, 66% federally funded.
