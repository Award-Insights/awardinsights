South Carolina State University

Orangeburg, South Carolina · Public · Carnegie R2

South Carolina State University received $54M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 62% of it from the Department of Agriculture. In FY2026 it received $5.4M (still being revised), against $15M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $17M in FY2025, 74% federally funded.
