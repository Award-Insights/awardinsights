Wayne State University

Detroit, Michigan · Public · Carnegie R1

Wayne State University received $514M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 72% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $83M (still being revised), against $113M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $292M in FY2025, 48% federally funded.
