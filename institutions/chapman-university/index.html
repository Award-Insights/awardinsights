Chapman University

Orange, California · Private nonprofit · Carnegie R2

Chapman University received $46M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 68% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $7.3M (still being revised), against $9.0M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $46M in FY2025, 28% federally funded.
