Virginia Commonwealth University

Richmond, Virginia · Public · Carnegie R1

Virginia Commonwealth University received $821M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 68% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $146M (still being revised), against $167M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $572M in FY2025, 40% federally funded.
