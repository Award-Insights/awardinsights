Scripps Research

La Jolla, California

Scripps Research received $943M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 97% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $163M (still being revised), against $204M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $450M in FY2025, 50% federally funded.
