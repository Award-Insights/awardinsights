University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill

Chapel Hill, North Carolina · Public · Carnegie R1

University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill received $3.50B in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 74% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $710M (still being revised), against $719M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $1.66B in FY2025, 59% federally funded.

The sources behind this page, and which recipients its award figures include

Recipients counted

Federal, NIH and NSF count awards made to these recipients, named as the records name them. Money University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill receives as a subaward from another organization isn't among them.
