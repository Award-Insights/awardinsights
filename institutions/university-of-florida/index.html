University of Florida

Gainesville, Florida · Public · Carnegie R1

University of Florida received $2.37B in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 57% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $442M (still being revised), against $485M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $1.33B in FY2025, 46% federally funded.

What’s counted

The sources behind this page, and which recipients its award figures include

Sources

  • Federal awards: USAspending.gov, prime grants, cooperative agreements and contracts, FY2022 through September 30, 2026
  • NIH awards: NIH RePORTER
  • NSF awards: NSF's award records
  • R&D spending: NSF's HERD survey, as the institution reports it

Recipients counted

Federal, NIH and NSF count awards made to these recipients, named as the records name them. Money University of Florida receives as a subaward from another organization isn't among them.
