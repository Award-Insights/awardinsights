CUNY Bernard M Baruch College

Data note

New York, New York · Public · Carnegie RCU

CUNY Bernard M Baruch College received $12M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 46% of it from the National Science Foundation. In FY2026 it received $1.2M (still being revised), against $3.3M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $11M in FY2025, 30% federally funded.

What’s counted

The sources behind this page, and which recipients its award figures include

Compared with HERD

The Research Foundation of CUNY files some federal awards under recipient IDs that aren't tied to one campus, which Federal counts with the CUNY System Office, so a campus can show less here than it reports to HERD.

Sources

  • Federal awards: USAspending.gov, prime grants, cooperative agreements and contracts, FY2022 through September 30, 2026
  • NIH awards: NIH RePORTER
  • NSF awards: NSF's award records
  • R&D spending: NSF's HERD survey, as the institution reports it
  • Research designation: Carnegie Classification, 2025

Recipients counted

Federal, NIH and NSF count awards made to these recipients, named as the records name them. Money CUNY Bernard M Baruch College receives as a subaward from another organization isn't among them.
