Miami University-Oxford

Oxford, Ohio · Public · Carnegie R2

Miami University-Oxford received $56M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 38% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $9.5M (still being revised), against $12M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $55M in FY2025, 20% federally funded.
