Bradley University

Peoria, Illinois · Private nonprofit

Bradley University received $5.9M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 98% of it from the National Science Foundation. In FY2026 it received $139K (still being revised), against $1.1M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $2.4M in FY2025, 80% federally funded.
