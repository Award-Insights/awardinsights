Tennessee College of Applied Technology-Morristown

Morristown, Tennessee

Tennessee College of Applied Technology-Morristown received $268K in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 67% of it from the Department of Agriculture. In FY2026 it received $100K (still being revised), against $27K a year in FY2022–24.
