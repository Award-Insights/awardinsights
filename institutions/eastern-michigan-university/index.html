Eastern Michigan University

Ypsilanti, Michigan · Public · Carnegie R2

Eastern Michigan University received $20M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 39% of it from the Department of Education. In FY2026 it received $1.8M (still being revised), against $5.0M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $16M in FY2025, 27% federally funded.
