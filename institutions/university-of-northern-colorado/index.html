University of Northern Colorado

Greeley, Colorado · Public · Carnegie R2

University of Northern Colorado received $24M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 43% of it from the Department of Education. In FY2026 it received $3.4M (still being revised), against $4.7M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $9.3M in FY2025, 24% federally funded.
