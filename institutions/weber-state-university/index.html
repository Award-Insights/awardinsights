Weber State University

Ogden, Utah · Public

Weber State University received $18M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 62% of it from the Department of Education. In FY2026 it received $2.4M (still being revised), against $4.1M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $11M in FY2025, 25% federally funded.
