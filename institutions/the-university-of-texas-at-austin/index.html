The University of Texas at Austin

Austin, Texas · Public · Carnegie R1

The University of Texas at Austin received $2.93B in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 30% of it from the National Science Foundation. In FY2026 it received $638M (still being revised), against $550M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $1.37B in FY2025, 62% federally funded.
