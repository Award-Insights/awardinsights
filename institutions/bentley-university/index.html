Bentley University

Waltham, Massachusetts · Private nonprofit

Bentley University received $822K in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 67% of it from the National Science Foundation. In FY2026 it received $22K (still being revised), against $267K a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $4.6M in FY2025, 16% federally funded.
