CUNY Queens College

Data note

Queens, New York · Public · Carnegie RCU

CUNY Queens College received $49M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 41% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $7.3M (still being revised), against $11M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $36M in FY2025, 57% federally funded.

What’s counted

The sources behind this page, and which recipients its award figures include

Compared with HERD

The Research Foundation of CUNY files some federal awards under recipient IDs that aren't tied to one campus, which Federal counts with the CUNY System Office, so a campus can show less here than it reports to HERD.

Sources

  • Federal awards: USAspending.gov, prime grants, cooperative agreements and contracts, FY2022 through September 30, 2026
  • NIH awards: NIH RePORTER
  • NSF awards: NSF's award records
  • R&D spending: NSF's HERD survey, as the institution reports it
  • Research designation: Carnegie Classification, 2025

Recipients counted

Federal, NIH and NSF count awards made to these recipients, named as the records name them. Money CUNY Queens College receives as a subaward from another organization isn't among them.

  • RESEARCH FOUNDATION OF THE CITY UNIVERSITY OF NEW YORKFederal · NIH · NSFAlso recorded as QUEENS COLLEGE; CUNY QUEENS COLLEGEThe Research Foundation for CUNY holds awards for CUNY campuses, each under its own recipient ID; this one is CUNY Queens College's.
