Midwestern State University

Wichita Falls, Texas · Public

Midwestern State University received $5.7M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 47% of it from the Department of Education. In FY2026 it received $1.4M (still being revised), against $1.1M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $1.4M in FY2025, 49% federally funded.
