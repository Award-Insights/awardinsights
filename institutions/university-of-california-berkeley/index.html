University of California-Berkeley

Data note

Berkeley, California · Public · Carnegie R1

University of California-Berkeley received $2.05B in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 35% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $381M (still being revised), against $416M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $1.19B in FY2025, 45% federally funded.

What’s counted

The sources behind this page, and which recipients its award figures include

Sources

  • Federal awards: USAspending.gov, prime grants, cooperative agreements and contracts, FY2022 through September 30, 2026
  • NIH awards: NIH RePORTER
  • NSF awards: NSF's award records
  • R&D spending: NSF's HERD survey, as the institution reports it
  • Research designation: Carnegie Classification, 2025

Recipients counted

Federal, NIH and NSF count awards made to these recipients, named as the records name them. Money University of California-Berkeley receives as a subaward from another organization isn't among them.

  • REGENTS OF THE UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA, THEFederal · NIH · NSFAlso recorded as UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA BERKELEY; UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA-BERKELEY
  • UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA, BERKELEYFederal

Counted elsewhere or left out

  • Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory, which the University of California manages for the Department of Energy, files under the name Regents of the University of California, Berkeley, with its own recipient ID. National labs are federally funded R&D centers (FFRDCs), which HERD leaves out, so its awards aren't counted here.

Something counted wrongly? Write to info@awardinsights.org.

* FY2026 is complete through September 30, 2026 but still being revised as agencies report. Figures follow the same rules for every institution, so they can differ from the institution's own reports, and each tool counts differently: why.
