Elizabeth City State University

Elizabeth City, North Carolina · Public

Elizabeth City State University received $27M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 41% of it from the Department of Education. In FY2026 it received $5.3M (still being revised), against $5.7M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $1.1M in FY2025, 52% federally funded.
