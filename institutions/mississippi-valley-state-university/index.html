Mississippi Valley State University

Itta Bena, Mississippi · Public

Mississippi Valley State University received $10M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 91% of it from the Department of Education. In FY2026 it received $1.8M (still being revised), against $2.2M a year in FY2022–24.
