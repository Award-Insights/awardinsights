Washington State University

Pullman, Washington · Public · Carnegie R1

Washington State University received $722M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 26% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $121M (still being revised), against $164M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $462M in FY2025, 48% federally funded.
