The University of Texas at Tyler

Tyler, Texas · Public · Carnegie R2

The University of Texas at Tyler received $52M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 52% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $9.0M (still being revised), against $11M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $38M in FY2025, 45% federally funded.

Recipients counted

Federal, NIH and NSF count awards made to these recipients, named as the records name them. Money The University of Texas at Tyler receives as a subaward from another organization isn't among them.
