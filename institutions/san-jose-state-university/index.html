San Jose State University

San Jose, California · Public · Carnegie R2

San Jose State University received $199M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 47% of it from NASA. In FY2026 it received $37M (still being revised), against $37M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $94M in FY2025, 46% federally funded.

The sources behind this page, and which recipients its award figures include

Recipients counted
