Uganda Virus Research Institute

Uganda Virus Research Institute received $26M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 86% of it from the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. In FY2026 it received $4.2M (still being revised), against $6.0M a year in FY2022–24.
