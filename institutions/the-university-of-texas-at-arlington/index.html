The University of Texas at Arlington

Arlington, Texas · Public · Carnegie R1

The University of Texas at Arlington received $307M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 21% of it from the National Science Foundation. In FY2026 it received $78M (still being revised), against $58M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $196M in FY2025, 32% federally funded.
