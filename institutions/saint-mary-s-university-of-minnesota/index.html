Saint Mary's University of Minnesota

Winona, Minnesota · Private nonprofit

Saint Mary's University of Minnesota received $5.7M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 73% of it from the Department of Interior. In FY2026 it received $160K (still being revised), against $1.6M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $1.5M in FY2025, 54% federally funded.
