University of North Carolina at Charlotte

Charlotte, North Carolina · Public · Carnegie R1

University of North Carolina at Charlotte received $148M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 40% of it from the National Science Foundation. In FY2026 it received $32M (still being revised), against $31M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $96M in FY2025, 35% federally funded.

Recipients counted
