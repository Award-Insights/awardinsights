New Mexico Highlands University

Las Vegas, New Mexico · Public · Carnegie RCU

New Mexico Highlands University received $23M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 45% of it from the Department of Agriculture. In FY2026 it received $2.5M (still being revised), against $6.2M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $3.5M in FY2025, 84% federally funded.
