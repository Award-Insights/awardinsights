Albany State University

Albany, Georgia · Public

Albany State University received $25M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 36% of it from the Department of Education. In FY2026 it received $4.8M (still being revised), against $5.4M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $2.2M in FY2025, 77% federally funded.
