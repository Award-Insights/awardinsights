The University of Texas MD Anderson Cancer Center

Houston, Texas · Public · Carnegie RCU

The University of Texas MD Anderson Cancer Center received $1.23B in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 81% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $243M (still being revised), against $234M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $1.45B in FY2025, 19% federally funded.

Recipients counted

Federal, NIH and NSF count awards made to these recipients, named as the records name them. Money The University of Texas MD Anderson Cancer Center receives as a subaward from another organization isn't among them.
