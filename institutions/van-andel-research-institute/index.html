Van Andel Research Institute

Grand Rapids, Michigan

Van Andel Research Institute received $141M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 96% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $29M (still being revised), against $28M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $92M in FY2025, 35% federally funded.
