Florida State University

Tallahassee, Florida · Public · Carnegie R1

Florida State University received $884M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 36% of it from the National Science Foundation. In FY2026 it received $178M (still being revised), against $179M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $488M in FY2025, 43% federally funded.
