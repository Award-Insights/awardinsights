Massachusetts Institute of Technology

Cambridge, Massachusetts · Private nonprofit · Carnegie R1

Massachusetts Institute of Technology received $2.19B in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 27% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $407M (still being revised), against $449M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $1.09B in FY2025, 57% federally funded.

Recipients counted

Federal, NIH and NSF count awards made to these recipients, named as the records name them. Money Massachusetts Institute of Technology receives as a subaward from another organization isn't among them.
