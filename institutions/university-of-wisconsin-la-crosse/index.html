University of Wisconsin-La Crosse

La Crosse, Wisconsin · Public · Carnegie RCU

University of Wisconsin-La Crosse received $10M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 49% of it from the Department of Education. In FY2026 it received $1.3M (still being revised), against $2.2M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $3.2M in FY2025, 34% federally funded.

What’s counted

The sources behind this page, and which recipients its award figures include

Sources

  • Federal awards: USAspending.gov, prime grants, cooperative agreements and contracts, FY2022 through September 30, 2026
  • NIH awards: NIH RePORTER
  • NSF awards: NSF's award records
  • R&D spending: NSF's HERD survey, as the institution reports it
  • Research designation: Carnegie Classification, 2025

Recipients counted

Federal, NIH and NSF count awards made to these recipients, named as the records name them. Money University of Wisconsin-La Crosse receives as a subaward from another organization isn't among them.

  • UNIVERSITY OF WISCONSIN SYSTEMFederal · NIH · NSFAlso recorded as UNIVERSITY OF WISCONSIN LA CROSSE; UNIVERSITY OF WISCONSIN-LA CROSSERegistered under the legal name of its system or governing board; IPEDS lists this recipient ID for University of Wisconsin-La Crosse.
