Long Beach City College

Long Beach, California · Public

Long Beach City College received $54M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 47% of it from the Small Business Administration. In FY2026 it received $8.7M (still being revised), against $13M a year in FY2022–24.
