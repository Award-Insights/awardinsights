The University of Tennessee-Knoxville

Knoxville, Tennessee · Public · Carnegie R1

The University of Tennessee-Knoxville received $719M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 20% of it from the Department of Defense. In FY2026 it received $132M (still being revised), against $153M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $413M in FY2025, 44% federally funded.

What’s counted

The sources behind this page, and which recipients its award figures include

Sources

  • Federal awards: USAspending.gov, prime grants, cooperative agreements and contracts, FY2022 through September 30, 2026
  • NIH awards: NIH RePORTER

Recipients counted

Federal, NIH and NSF count awards made to these recipients, named as the records name them. Money The University of Tennessee-Knoxville receives as a subaward from another organization isn't among them.
