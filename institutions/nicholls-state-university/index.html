Nicholls State University

Thibodaux, Louisiana · Public

Nicholls State University received $2.2M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 93% of it from other HHS divisions. In FY2026 it received $2.0M (still being revised), against $52K a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $734K in FY2025, 60% federally funded.
