Rutgers University-Newark

Data note

Newark, New Jersey · Public · Carnegie R2

Rutgers University-Newark received $162M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 48% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $18M (still being revised), against $40M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $27M in FY2025, 70% federally funded.

What’s counted

The sources behind this page, and which recipients its award figures include

Sources

  • Federal awards: USAspending.gov, prime grants, cooperative agreements and contracts, FY2022 through September 30, 2026
  • NIH awards: NIH RePORTER
  • NSF awards: NSF's award records
  • R&D spending: NSF's HERD survey, as the institution reports it
  • Research designation: Carnegie Classification, 2025

Recipients counted

Federal, NIH and NSF count awards made to these recipients, named as the records name them. Money Rutgers University-Newark receives as a subaward from another organization isn't among them.

  • RUTGERS, THE STATE UNIVERSITYFederal · NIH · NSFAlso recorded as RUTGERS THE STATE UNIV OF NJ NEWARK; RUTGERS UNIVERSITY NEWARKIPEDS lists this recipient ID for Rutgers University-Newark.
  • RUTGERS, THE STATE UNIVERSITYFederal · NIHAlso recorded as RBHS-NEW JERSEY MEDICAL SCHOOL; RBHS-SCHOOL/ HEALTH RELATED PROFESSIONS

Counted elsewhere or left out

  • Rutgers files many awards, including Rutgers Biomedical and Health Sciences, under one registration at its Newark address. HERD reports that research under Rutgers University-New Brunswick, so those awards count there.

Something counted wrongly? Write to info@awardinsights.org.
