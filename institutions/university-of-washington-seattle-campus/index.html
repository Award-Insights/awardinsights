University of Washington-Seattle Campus

Seattle, Washington · Public · Carnegie R1

University of Washington-Seattle Campus received $5.14B in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 53% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $943M (still being revised), against $1.08B a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $1.84B in FY2025, 67% federally funded.
