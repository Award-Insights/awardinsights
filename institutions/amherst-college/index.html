Amherst College

Amherst, Massachusetts · Private nonprofit · Carnegie RCU

Amherst College received $11M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 76% of it from the National Science Foundation. In FY2026 it received $2.1M (still being revised), against $2.1M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $7.7M in FY2025, 33% federally funded.
