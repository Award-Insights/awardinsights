Southern Methodist University

Dallas, Texas · Private nonprofit · Carnegie R1

Southern Methodist University received $141M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 29% of it from the Department of Education. In FY2026 it received $17M (still being revised), against $23M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $70M in FY2025, 34% federally funded.
