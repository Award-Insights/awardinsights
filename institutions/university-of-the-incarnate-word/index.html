University of the Incarnate Word

San Antonio, Texas · Private nonprofit

University of the Incarnate Word received $20M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 42% of it from the Department of Education. In FY2026 it received $3.5M (still being revised), against $4.8M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $2.2M in FY2025, 44% federally funded.
