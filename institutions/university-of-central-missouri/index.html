University of Central Missouri

Warrensburg, Missouri · Public

University of Central Missouri received $3.8M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 64% of it from the Department of Education. In FY2026 it received $838K (still being revised), against $797K a year in FY2022–24.
