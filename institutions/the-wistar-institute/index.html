The Wistar Institute

Philadelphia, Pennsylvania

The Wistar Institute received $226M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 95% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $53M (still being revised), against $44M a year in FY2022–24.
