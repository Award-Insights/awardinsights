Vassar College

Poughkeepsie, New York · Private nonprofit · Carnegie RCU

Vassar College received $6.0M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 59% of it from the National Science Foundation. In FY2026 it received $1.4M (still being revised), against $1.5M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $3.2M in FY2025, 45% federally funded.
