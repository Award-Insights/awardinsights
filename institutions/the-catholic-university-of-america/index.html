The Catholic University of America

Washington, District of Columbia · Private nonprofit · Carnegie R1

The Catholic University of America received $137M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 75% of it from NASA. In FY2026 it received $28M (still being revised), against $26M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $61M in FY2025, 74% federally funded.
