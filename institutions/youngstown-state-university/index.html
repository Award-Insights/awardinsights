Youngstown State University

Youngstown, Ohio · Public · Carnegie RCU

Youngstown State University received $8.7M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 34% of it from the National Science Foundation. In FY2026 it received $902K (still being revised), against $2.0M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $2.2M in FY2025, 87% federally funded.
