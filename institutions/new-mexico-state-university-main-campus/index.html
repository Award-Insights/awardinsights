New Mexico State University-Main Campus

Las Cruces, New Mexico · Public · Carnegie R1

New Mexico State University-Main Campus received $372M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 26% of it from the Department of Agriculture. In FY2026 it received $51M (still being revised), against $83M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $153M in FY2025, 58% federally funded.
