University of Oklahoma-Health Sciences Center

Oklahoma City, Oklahoma · Public · Carnegie R2

University of Oklahoma-Health Sciences Center received $491M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 73% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $95M (still being revised), against $100M a year in FY2022–24.

What’s counted

The sources behind this page, and which recipients its award figures include

Sources

  • Federal awards: USAspending.gov, prime grants, cooperative agreements and contracts, FY2022 through September 30, 2026
  • NIH awards: NIH RePORTER
  • NSF awards: NSF's award records
  • R&D spending: NSF's HERD survey, as the institution reports it, together with other campuses as University of Oklahoma (Norman + Health Sciences)
  • Research designation: Carnegie Classification, 2025

Recipients counted

Federal, NIH and NSF count awards made to these recipients, named as the records name them. Money University of Oklahoma-Health Sciences Center receives as a subaward from another organization isn't among them.

  • UNIVERSITY OF OKLAHOMAFederal · NIH · NSFAlso recorded as UNIVERSITY OF OKLAHOMA HLTH SCIENCES CTR; UNIVERSITY OF OKLAHOMA HEALTH SCIENCES CENTER

Something counted wrongly? Write to info@awardinsights.org.
