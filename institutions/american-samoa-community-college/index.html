American Samoa Community College

Pago Pago, American Samoa · Public

American Samoa Community College received $19M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 27% of it from the Department of Interior. In FY2026 it received $664K (still being revised), against $5.7M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $1.3M in FY2025, 97% federally funded.
