University of St Thomas

Saint Paul, Minnesota · Private nonprofit · Carnegie RCU

University of St Thomas received $25M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 34% of it from the Department of Defense. In FY2026 it received $1.3M (still being revised), against $6.6M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $15M in FY2025, 81% federally funded.
