University of Missouri-Columbia

Columbia, Missouri · Public · Carnegie R1

University of Missouri-Columbia received $892M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 42% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $153M (still being revised), against $194M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $553M in FY2025, 47% federally funded.

The sources behind this page, and which recipients its award figures include

Sources

Recipients counted

Federal, NIH and NSF count awards made to these recipients, named as the records name them. Money University of Missouri-Columbia receives as a subaward from another organization isn't among them.
