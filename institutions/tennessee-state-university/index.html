Tennessee State University

Nashville, Tennessee · Public · Carnegie R2

Tennessee State University received $149M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 46% of it from the Department of Agriculture. In FY2026 it received $24M (still being revised), against $35M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $38M in FY2025, 68% federally funded.
