California State University-Chico

Chico, California · Public · Carnegie RCU

California State University-Chico received $80M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 54% of it from the Department of Education. In FY2026 it received $14M (still being revised), against $19M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $9.7M in FY2025, 29% federally funded.
