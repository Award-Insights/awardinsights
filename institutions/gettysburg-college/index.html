Gettysburg College

Gettysburg, Pennsylvania · Private nonprofit

Gettysburg College received $2.6M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 52% of it from the National Science Foundation. In FY2026 it received $0 (still being revised), against $530K a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $1.0M in FY2025, 48% federally funded.
