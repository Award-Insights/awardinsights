Georgetown University

Washington, District of Columbia · Private nonprofit · Carnegie R1

Georgetown University received $651M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 43% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $123M (still being revised), against $144M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $366M in FY2025, 35% federally funded.
