Pennsylvania College of Technology

Williamsport, Pennsylvania · Public

Pennsylvania College of Technology received $12M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 32% of it from the Department of Labor. In FY2026 it received $475K (still being revised), against $3.8M a year in FY2022–24.
