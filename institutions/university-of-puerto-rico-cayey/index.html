University of Puerto Rico-Cayey

Cayey, Puerto Rico · Public

University of Puerto Rico-Cayey received $5.1M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 37% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $967K (still being revised), against $1.4M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $1.6M in FY2025, 51% federally funded.
