George Washington University

Washington, District of Columbia · Private nonprofit · Carnegie R1

George Washington University received $652M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 64% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $122M (still being revised), against $136M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $406M in FY2025, 47% federally funded.
