Syracuse University

Syracuse, New York · Private nonprofit · Carnegie R1

Syracuse University received $217M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 32% of it from the National Science Foundation. In FY2026 it received $28M (still being revised), against $47M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $201M in FY2025, 29% federally funded.
