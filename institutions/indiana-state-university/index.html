Indiana State University

Terre Haute, Indiana · Public

Indiana State University received $7.0M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 24% of it from the Health Resources and Services Administration. In FY2026 it received $1.7M (still being revised), against $1.5M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $1.3M in FY2025, 68% federally funded.
