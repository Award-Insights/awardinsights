Western University of Health Sciences

Pomona, California · Private nonprofit · Carnegie RCU

Western University of Health Sciences received $35M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 39% of it from the Health Resources and Services Administration. In FY2026 it received $5.5M (still being revised), against $7.5M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $9.4M in FY2025, 74% federally funded.
