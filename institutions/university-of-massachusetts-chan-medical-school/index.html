University of Massachusetts Chan Medical School

Worcester, Massachusetts · Public · Carnegie R2

University of Massachusetts Chan Medical School received $1.35B in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 69% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $301M (still being revised), against $259M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $356M in FY2025, 67% federally funded.
