Carnegie Mellon University

Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania · Private nonprofit · Carnegie R1

Carnegie Mellon University received $919M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 35% of it from the National Science Foundation. In FY2026 it received $160M (still being revised), against $191M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $558M in FY2025, 52% federally funded.
