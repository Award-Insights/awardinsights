Temple University

Philadelphia, Pennsylvania · Public · Carnegie R1

Temple University received $498M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 69% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $78M (still being revised), against $109M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $334M in FY2025, 50% federally funded.

What’s counted

The sources behind this page, and which recipients its award figures include

Sources

  • Federal awards: USAspending.gov, prime grants, cooperative agreements and contracts, FY2022 through September 30, 2026
  • NIH awards: NIH RePORTER

  • R&D spending: NSF's HERD survey, as the institution reports it

Recipients counted

Federal, NIH and NSF count awards made to these recipients, named as the records name them. Money Temple University receives as a subaward from another organization isn't among them.
