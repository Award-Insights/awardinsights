Southern University and A & M College

Data note

Baton Rouge, Louisiana · Public · Carnegie R2

Southern University and A & M College received $114M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 34% of it from the Department of Agriculture. In FY2026 it received $19M (still being revised), against $27M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $15M in FY2025, 80% federally funded.

What’s counted

The sources behind this page, and which recipients its award figures include

Sources

  • Federal awards: USAspending.gov, prime grants, cooperative agreements and contracts, FY2022 through September 30, 2026
  • NIH awards: NIH RePORTER
  • NSF awards: NSF's award records
  • R&D spending: NSF's HERD survey, as the institution reports it
  • Research designation: Carnegie Classification, 2025

Recipients counted

Federal, NIH and NSF count awards made to these recipients, named as the records name them. Money Southern University and A & M College receives as a subaward from another organization isn't among them.

  • SOUTHERN UNIVERSITY AGRICULTURAL & MECHANICAL COLLEGEFederalSouthern University A&M College's registration in Baton Rouge. IPEDS lists it for Southern University Law Center, but it carries Southern University's awards.
  • SOUTHERN UNIVERSITY AND A & M COLLEGEFederal · NIH · NSFAlso recorded as SOUTHERN UNIV A&M COL BATON ROUGE; SOUTHERN UNIVERSITY
  • SOUTHERN UNIVERSITY AGRICULTURAL & MECHANICAL COLLEGEFederalSouthern University and A & M College's agricultural, forestry or extension unit, registered under its own recipient ID.
