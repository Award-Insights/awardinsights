Texas A&M University-Kingsville

Kingsville, Texas · Public · Carnegie R2

Texas A&M University-Kingsville received $107M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 37% of it from the Department of Agriculture. In FY2026 it received $14M (still being revised), against $28M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $42M in FY2025, 42% federally funded.
