Northern Kentucky University

Highland Heights, Kentucky · Public · Carnegie RCU

Northern Kentucky University received $23M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 29% of it from the Health Resources and Services Administration. In FY2026 it received $3.3M (still being revised), against $5.2M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $3.5M in FY2025, 69% federally funded.
