What’s counted

The sources behind this page, and which recipients its award figures include

Compared with HERD

Eastern Virginia Medical School merged into Old Dominion University in July 2024. Its federal awards count here through FY2024, as HERD reported it, and with Old Dominion from FY2025.

Federal, NIH and NSF count awards made to these recipients, named as the records name them. Money Eastern Virginia Medical School receives as a subaward from another organization isn't among them.
