Carthage College

Kenosha, Wisconsin · Private nonprofit

Carthage College received $9.2M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 79% of it from NASA. In FY2026 it received $1.2M (still being revised), against $2.0M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $1.7M in FY2025, 94% federally funded.
