Bryant University

Smithfield, Rhode Island · Private nonprofit

Bryant University received $3.9M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 68% of it from the Small Business Administration. In FY2026 it received $2.0M (still being revised), against $499K a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $388K in FY2025, 53% federally funded.
