West Virginia University at Parkersburg

Parkersburg, West Virginia · Public

West Virginia University at Parkersburg received $11M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 81% of it from the Department of Labor. In FY2026 it received $2.8M (still being revised), against $2.6M a year in FY2022–24.
