Lehigh University

Bethlehem, Pennsylvania · Private nonprofit · Carnegie R1

Lehigh University received $174M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 42% of it from the National Science Foundation. In FY2026 it received $20M (still being revised), against $41M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $64M in FY2025, 74% federally funded.
