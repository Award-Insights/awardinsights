New Mexico Institute of Mining and Technology

Socorro, New Mexico · Public · Carnegie RCU

New Mexico Institute of Mining and Technology received $308M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 28% of it from the Department of Energy. In FY2026 it received $9.6M (still being revised), against $78M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $80M in FY2025, 71% federally funded.

Recipients counted

Federal, NIH and NSF count awards made to these recipients, named as the records name them. Money New Mexico Institute of Mining and Technology receives as a subaward from another organization isn't among them.
