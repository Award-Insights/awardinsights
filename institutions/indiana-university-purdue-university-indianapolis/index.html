What’s counted

The sources behind this page, and which recipients its award figures include

Compared with HERD

IUPUI's schools share Indiana University's recipient ID, so most of their federal money appears under Indiana University–Bloomington here.

Sources

  • R&D spending: NSF's HERD survey, as the institution reports it
  • Research designation: Carnegie Classification, 2025

Counted elsewhere or left out

  • Indiana University's School of Medicine files its awards under the name Trustees of Indiana University, Indianapolis. HERD has reported the school under Indiana University Bloomington since FY2015, so those awards count there. IU Indianapolis's other schools file under the same registration, so the award tools show no awards here.
