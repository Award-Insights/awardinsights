University of Alabama in Huntsville

Huntsville, Alabama · Public · Carnegie R2

University of Alabama in Huntsville received $342M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 49% of it from NASA. In FY2026 it received $65M (still being revised), against $65M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $163M in FY2025, 74% federally funded.

Recipients counted

Federal, NIH and NSF count awards made to these recipients, named as the records name them. Money University of Alabama in Huntsville receives as a subaward from another organization isn't among them.
