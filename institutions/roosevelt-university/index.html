Roosevelt University

Chicago, Illinois · Private nonprofit

Roosevelt University received $22M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 89% of it from the Department of Education. In FY2026 it received $2.4M (still being revised), against $4.8M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $2.5M in FY2025, 51% federally funded.
