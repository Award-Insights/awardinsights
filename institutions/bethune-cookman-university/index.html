Bethune-Cookman University

Daytona Beach, Florida · Private nonprofit

Bethune-Cookman University received $5.7M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 28% of it from the National Science Foundation. In FY2026 it received $1.8M (still being revised), against $823K a year in FY2022–24.
