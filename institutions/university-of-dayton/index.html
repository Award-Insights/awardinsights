University of Dayton

Dayton, Ohio · Private nonprofit · Carnegie R1

University of Dayton received $1.08B in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 77% of it from the Department of Defense. In FY2026 it received $196M (still being revised), against $210M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $257M in FY2025, 96% federally funded.
