University of Massachusetts-Boston

Boston, Massachusetts · Public · Carnegie R1

University of Massachusetts-Boston received $155M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 39% of it from the Department of Education. In FY2026 it received $26M (still being revised), against $33M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $76M in FY2025, 43% federally funded.
