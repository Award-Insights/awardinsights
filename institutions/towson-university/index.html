Towson University

Towson, Maryland · Public · Carnegie RCU

Towson University received $23M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 33% of it from the National Science Foundation. In FY2026 it received $4.1M (still being revised), against $4.6M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $19M in FY2025, 30% federally funded.
