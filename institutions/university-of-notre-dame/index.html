University of Notre Dame

Notre Dame, Indiana · Private nonprofit · Carnegie R1

University of Notre Dame received $551M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 33% of it from the National Science Foundation. In FY2026 it received $97M (still being revised), against $116M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $356M in FY2025, 43% federally funded.
