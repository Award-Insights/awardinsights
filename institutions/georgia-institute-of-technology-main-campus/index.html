Georgia Institute of Technology-Main Campus

Atlanta, Georgia · Public · Carnegie R1

Georgia Institute of Technology-Main Campus received $4.37B in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 75% of it from the Department of Defense. In FY2026 it received $760M (still being revised), against $894M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $1.66B in FY2025, 79% federally funded.

The sources behind this page, and which recipients its award figures include
