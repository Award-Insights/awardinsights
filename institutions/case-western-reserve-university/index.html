Case Western Reserve University

Data note

Cleveland, Ohio · Private nonprofit · Carnegie R1

Case Western Reserve University received $1.79B in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 87% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $344M (still being revised), against $370M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $691M in FY2025, 72% federally funded.

What’s counted

The sources behind this page, and which recipients its award figures include

Sources

Recipients counted

Federal, NIH and NSF count awards made to these recipients, named as the records name them. Money Case Western Reserve University receives as a subaward from another organization isn't among them.
