Grambling State University

Grambling, Louisiana · Public

Grambling State University received $22M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 44% of it from the Department of Education. In FY2026 it received $4.0M (still being revised), against $3.1M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $3.1M in FY2025, 100% federally funded.
