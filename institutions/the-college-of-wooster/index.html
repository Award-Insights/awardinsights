The College of Wooster

Wooster, Ohio · Private nonprofit

The College of Wooster received $2.0M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 44% of it from the National Science Foundation. In FY2026 it received $483K (still being revised), against $387K a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $2.8M in FY2025, 17% federally funded.
