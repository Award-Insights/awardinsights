University of Puerto Rico-Humacao

Humacao, Puerto Rico · Public

University of Puerto Rico-Humacao received $8.9M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 52% of it from the National Science Foundation. In FY2026 it received $325K (still being revised), against $2.6M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $2.6M in FY2025, 93% federally funded.
