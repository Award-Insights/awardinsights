Ohio State University-Main Campus

Columbus, Ohio · Public · Carnegie R1

Ohio State University-Main Campus received $2.32B in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 57% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $440M (still being revised), against $465M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $1.68B in FY2025, 48% federally funded.
