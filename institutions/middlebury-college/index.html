Middlebury College

Middlebury, Vermont · Private nonprofit · Carnegie RCU

Middlebury College received $22M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 31% of it from the National Science Foundation. In FY2026 it received $5.7M (still being revised), against $4.5M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $3.7M in FY2025, 83% federally funded.
