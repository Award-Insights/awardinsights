St Lawrence University

Canton, New York · Private nonprofit

St Lawrence University received $1.9M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 73% of it from the Department of Education. In FY2026 it received $1 (still being revised), against $415K a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $2.3M in FY2025, 6% federally funded.
