SUNY Polytechnic Institute

Data note

Utica, New York · Public · Carnegie RCU

SUNY Polytechnic Institute received $65M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 82% of it from the Department of Defense. In FY2026 it received $0 (still being revised), against $22M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $3.4M in FY2025, 33% federally funded.

What’s counted

The sources behind this page, and which recipients its award figures include

Sources

  • Federal awards: USAspending.gov, prime grants, cooperative agreements and contracts, FY2022 through September 30, 2026
  • NIH awards: NIH RePORTER
  • NSF awards: NSF's award records
  • R&D spending: NSF's HERD survey, as the institution reports it
  • Research designation: Carnegie Classification, 2025

Recipients counted

Federal, NIH and NSF count awards made to these recipients, named as the records name them. Money SUNY Polytechnic Institute receives as a subaward from another organization isn't among them.

  • THE RESEARCH FOUNDATION FOR THE STATE UNIVERSITY OF NEW YORKFederal · NIH · NSFAlso recorded as SUNY POLYTECHNIC INSTITUTEThe Research Foundation for SUNY's Albany registration for the nanotechnology research (the College of Nanoscale Science and Engineering and AIM Photonics) that moved from SUNY Poly to the University at Albany in 2023. As HERD reports it, awards through FY2023 count with SUNY Poly and from FY2024 with the University at Albany.
