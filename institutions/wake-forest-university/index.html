Wake Forest University

Winston-Salem, North Carolina · Private nonprofit · Carnegie R2

Wake Forest University received $981M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 79% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $201M (still being revised), against $190M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $403M in FY2025, 64% federally funded.

What’s counted

The sources behind this page, and which recipients its award figures include

Sources

  • Federal awards: USAspending.gov, prime grants, cooperative agreements and contracts, FY2022 through September 30, 2026
  • NIH awards: NIH RePORTER

  • R&D spending: NSF's HERD survey, as the institution reports it

Recipients counted

Federal, NIH and NSF count awards made to these recipients, named as the records name them. Money Wake Forest University receives as a subaward from another organization isn't among them.
