Kutztown University of Pennsylvania

Kutztown, Pennsylvania · Public

Kutztown University of Pennsylvania received $36M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 73% of it from the Small Business Administration. In FY2026 it received $7.7M (still being revised), against $6.8M a year in FY2022–24.
