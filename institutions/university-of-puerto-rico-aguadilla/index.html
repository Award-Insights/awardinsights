University of Puerto Rico-Aguadilla

Aguadilla, Puerto Rico · Public

University of Puerto Rico-Aguadilla received $7.2M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 38% of it from the Department of Education. In FY2026 it received $622K (still being revised), against $2.2M a year in FY2022–24.
