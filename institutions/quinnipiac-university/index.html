Quinnipiac University

Hamden, Connecticut · Private nonprofit

Quinnipiac University received $2.8M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 40% of it from the Department of Defense. In FY2026 it received $777K (still being revised), against $320K a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $2.5M in FY2025, 7% federally funded.
