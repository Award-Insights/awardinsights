MGH Institute of Health Professions

Boston, Massachusetts · Private nonprofit · Carnegie RCU

MGH Institute of Health Professions received $35M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 57% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $5.6M (still being revised), against $7.5M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $11M in FY2025, 89% federally funded.
