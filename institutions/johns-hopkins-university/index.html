Johns Hopkins University

Data note

Baltimore, Maryland · Private nonprofit · Carnegie R1

Johns Hopkins University received $16.20B in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 50% of it from the Department of Defense. In FY2026 it received $3.31B (still being revised), against $3.12B a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $4.53B in FY2025, 87% federally funded.

What’s counted

The sources behind this page, and which recipients its award figures include

Sources

  • Federal awards: USAspending.gov, prime grants, cooperative agreements and contracts, FY2022 through September 30, 2026
  • NIH awards: NIH RePORTER
  • NSF awards: NSF's award records
  • R&D spending: NSF's HERD survey, as the institution reports it
  • Research designation: Carnegie Classification, 2025

Recipients counted

Federal, NIH and NSF count awards made to these recipients, named as the records name them. Money Johns Hopkins University receives as a subaward from another organization isn't among them.

  • THE JOHNS HOPKINS UNIVERSITY APPLIED PHYSICS LABORATORY LLCFederalJohns Hopkins' Applied Physics Laboratory, a University Affiliated Research Center (UARC) for the Defense Department. HERD counts it with Johns Hopkins, so its awards count here.
  • THE JOHNS HOPKINS UNIVERSITYFederal · NIH · NSFAlso recorded as JOHNS HOPKINS UNIVERSITY
  • JOHNS HOPKINS UNIVERSITY, THEFederal
