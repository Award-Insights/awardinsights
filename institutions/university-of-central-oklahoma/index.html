University of Central Oklahoma

Edmond, Oklahoma · Public · Carnegie RCU

University of Central Oklahoma received $39M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 78% of it from the Department of Education. In FY2026 it received $10M (still being revised), against $6.9M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $6.9M in FY2025, 45% federally funded.
