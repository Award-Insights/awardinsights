University of New Orleans

New Orleans, Louisiana · Public · Carnegie R2

University of New Orleans received $108M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 56% of it from the Environmental Protection Agency. In FY2026 it received $13M (still being revised), against $25M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $21M in FY2025, 39% federally funded.

Recipients counted

Federal, NIH and NSF count awards made to these recipients, named as the records name them. Money University of New Orleans receives as a subaward from another organization isn't among them.
