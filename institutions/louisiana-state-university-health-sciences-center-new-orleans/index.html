Louisiana State University Health Sciences Center-New Orleans

New Orleans, Louisiana · Public · Carnegie R2

Louisiana State University Health Sciences Center-New Orleans received $201M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 64% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $38M (still being revised), against $40M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $82M in FY2025, 43% federally funded.

Recipients counted

Federal, NIH and NSF count awards made to these recipients, named as the records name them. Money Louisiana State University Health Sciences Center-New Orleans receives as a subaward from another organization isn't among them.
