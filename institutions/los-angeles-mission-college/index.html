Los Angeles Mission College

Sylmar, California · Public

Los Angeles Mission College received $11M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 83% of it from the Department of Education. In FY2026 it received $284K (still being revised), against $2.9M a year in FY2022–24.
