University of Utah

Salt Lake City, Utah · Public · Carnegie R1

University of Utah received $2.17B in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 61% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $414M (still being revised), against $451M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $817M in FY2025, 57% federally funded.

What’s counted

The sources behind this page, and which recipients its award figures include

Sources

  • Federal awards: USAspending.gov, prime grants, cooperative agreements and contracts, FY2022 through September 30, 2026

  • R&D spending: NSF's HERD survey, as the institution reports it

Recipients counted
