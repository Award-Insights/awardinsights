University of Hawaii at Hilo

Hilo, Hawaii · Public · Carnegie RCU

University of Hawaii at Hilo received $61M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 44% of it from the Department of Education. In FY2026 it received $10M (still being revised), against $13M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $9.1M in FY2025, 76% federally funded.

Recipients counted

Federal, NIH and NSF count awards made to these recipients, named as the records name them. Money University of Hawaii at Hilo receives as a subaward from another organization isn't among them.
