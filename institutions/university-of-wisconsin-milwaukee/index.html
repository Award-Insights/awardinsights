University of Wisconsin-Milwaukee

Milwaukee, Wisconsin · Public · Carnegie R1

University of Wisconsin-Milwaukee received $129M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 28% of it from the National Institutes of Health. In FY2026 it received $21M (still being revised), against $28M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $70M in FY2025, 38% federally funded.

What’s counted

The sources behind this page, and which recipients its award figures include

Sources

  • Federal awards: USAspending.gov, prime grants, cooperative agreements and contracts, FY2022 through September 30, 2026
  • NIH awards: NIH RePORTER
  • NSF awards: NSF's award records
  • R&D spending: NSF's HERD survey, as the institution reports it
  • Research designation: Carnegie Classification, 2025

Recipients counted

Federal, NIH and NSF count awards made to these recipients, named as the records name them. Money University of Wisconsin-Milwaukee receives as a subaward from another organization isn't among them.

  • UNIVERSITY OF WISCONSIN SYSTEMFederal · NIH · NSFAlso recorded as UNIVERSITY OF WISCONSIN MILWAUKEE; UNIVERSITY OF WISCONSIN-MILWAUKEERegistered under the legal name of its system or governing board; IPEDS lists this recipient ID for several institutions, and its awards count here.

Something counted wrongly? Write to info@awardinsights.org.

* FY2026 is complete through September 30, 2026 but still being revised as agencies report. Figures follow the same rules for every institution, so they can differ from the institution's own reports, and each tool counts differently: why.
