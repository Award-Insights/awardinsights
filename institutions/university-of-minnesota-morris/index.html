University of Minnesota-Morris

Morris, Minnesota · Public

University of Minnesota-Morris received $4.6M in federal grants, cooperative agreements and contracts in FY2022–26, 92% of it from the Department of Education. In FY2026 it received $745K (still being revised), against $1.2M a year in FY2022–24. Its R&D spending, as reported to NSF's HERD survey, was $769K in FY2025, 21% federally funded.
